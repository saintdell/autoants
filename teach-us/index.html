Teach us your trade

You know how the work really gets done. We'll pay $150 for an hour walking us through it.

autoants builds assistants and scheduling math for local businesses: dispatch, routing, cutting, shifts, follow-up. The software is the easy part. The hard part is knowing how a trade actually runs: where jobs slip, who decides, and what the owner would never hand to a computer. That's what we want to learn from you.

Who we're looking for

  • Owners, or people who used to run a shop, a crew, a dispatch desk or an estimating desk
  • Retired tradespeople who know how the business side worked
  • People who see many shops: counter staff, equipment reps, instructors (general knowledge only)

How it works

  1. Email us the four answers below.
  2. If it's a fit, we book an hour by phone or video, or come to you.
  3. You walk us through a real day and one job from first call to paid. We ask a lot of "what happens when that goes wrong?"
  4. We pay $150 within a week. You don't have to buy anything or introduce anyone.

The ground rules

  • Only your own business, or what you know from your career. Nothing you're bound to keep confidential: no employer's private information, customer lists or trade secrets. If a question touches that, we skip it.
  • Recorded only with your OK, so our notes are right. Ask us to cut anything.
  • Your name stays private. We never use your name or business name publicly without your written OK.
  • Introductions are optional. If you later introduce a business that signs up, our standard referral fee applies. It's never expected.
