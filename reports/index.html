Before you buy

Where it comes from
City of New Orleans open data (data.nola.gov and gis.nola.gov), read when you order. Public records can be incomplete, late or wrong, and older work can predate the online files. Confirm anything important before you rely on it. A report is not a title search, survey, appraisal or inspection.
How you get it
After you pay, the next page says your report is being prepared and shows the download link when it's ready. We email you the link too. The link works for 7 days.
Refunds
If we can't make your report (the address doesn't match one Orleans Parish parcel, the address is on the protected-records list, or there are too few permits), we refund you in full. If something in the report is wrong because we made a mistake, email hello@autoants.com.
