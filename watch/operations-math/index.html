How the free week works

  1. You send one normal past week: stops, cut lists or schedules.
  2. We watch your planner build one day, to learn the rules that aren't in the spreadsheet.
  3. We show our plan next to what you actually did. Every number is labeled modeled until it's measured on your records.
  4. Your files are used only for your plans. No customer names or phone numbers. Deleted within 30 days after you're done.

What we don't promise

  • No savings promised before it's measured.
  • The price is never tied to modeled savings.
  • Nothing reaches a driver or a machine without your planner's OK.
