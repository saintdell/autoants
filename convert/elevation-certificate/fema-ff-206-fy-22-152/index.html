What you get

  • The flood zone, map panel and base flood elevation (BFE)
  • The building diagram number and what it means in plain words

Before you use it

Not insurance advice
We restate the certificate. We don't quote, rate or say what your flood policy will cost or cover. Ask your insurance agent how this affects your premium.
Not a determination
The summary isn't a flood zone determination, a Letter of Map Amendment or a survey. The certificate and its certifier govern.
Property facts only
The owner's name and any policy number are read only so they can be left out of the summary and CSV.
Your file
Your file is processed and then deleted, whether the conversion works or not. We keep a one-line receipt (the time, the page count and a fingerprint of the file), never the file or its text.
How it reads
Form fields and the page layout first. Anything still missing can be read by a model that runs on our own machine, and every value it reads is marked "check this".
