Before you use it

Not an award
A bid tabulation lists the bids received. It isn't a notice of award; the agency checks each bid before any contract. Apparent low bid is as tabulated.
Public records
Bid tabulations are public records. We keep the facts (bidder, amounts) and a link to the agency's own page, never a copy of its PDF.
As printed
Amounts are as the agency printed them. An amount that can't be read (such as $56,7600.00) is marked, not guessed.
Your file
Your file is processed and then deleted, whether the conversion works or not. We keep a one-line receipt (the time, the page count and a fingerprint of the file), never the file or its text.
How it reads
Form fields and the page layout first. Anything still missing can be read by a model that runs on our own machine, and every value it reads is marked "check this".
