HUD NSPIRE or REAC inspection report to a work-order list. Upload the PDF; get the numbers back, each one marked by where it was read.

What you get

  • One numbered work order per deficiency, life-threatening first, then severe, moderate and low
  • Each one assigned to a trade (electrical, plumbing, fire and life safety and so on)
  • The report's correction date, or the usual timeframe for its severity, marked to confirm
  • A count by trade and severity for the maintenance meeting
  • A spreadsheet and a printable list per trade
  • Optional: a draft hand-off to licensed contractors through our Referral Desk, with the referral fee disclosed

Before you use it

Not an inspection
This sorts the deficiencies in the inspection report you upload. It doesn't inspect anything and doesn't say whether a property will pass.
Not HUD
Not affiliated with or endorsed by HUD or REAC. Correction dates on your report and HUD's notices govern.
Referral fee
If you use the Referral Desk hand-off, contractors on that list pay Lane Davis II a referral fee. That never changes who's shown or what you pay. Check licenses yourself at lslbc.louisiana.gov.
Your file
Your file is processed and then deleted, whether the conversion works or not. We keep a one-line receipt (the time, the page count and a fingerprint of the file), never the file or its text.
How it reads
Form fields and the page layout first. Anything still missing can be read by a model that runs on our own machine, and every value it reads is marked "check this".
