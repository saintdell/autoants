Before you use it

Tenant names off by default
The workbook keeps the unit and whether it's occupied, not who lives there. Names can be included only by switching it on, and the workbook then carries a privacy note.
As printed
Figures are as printed on the owner's rent roll. Not an estoppel, an audit or an appraisal; confirm against leases and bank records.
Property facts only
Nothing about any resident beyond the lease figures, ever.
Your file
Your file is processed and then deleted, whether the conversion works or not. We keep a one-line receipt (the time, the page count and a fingerprint of the file), never the file or its text.
How it reads
Form fields and the page layout first. Anything still missing can be read by a model that runs on our own machine, and every value it reads is marked "check this".
