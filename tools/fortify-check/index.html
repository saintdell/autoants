Can I get a Fortify Homes grant?

It depends. The main rules: the home is in one of the 25 listed parishes, it's your primary residence with a homestead exemption, you have an in-force policy that covers wind, and you have flood insurance if the home is in a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area. Condos and mobile homes don't qualify, and a dry-stack foundation needs a retrofit first. This check is a first pass; LDI and its evaluator check more. Then it's a lottery: meeting the rules gets you into the drawing, not a grant.

The rules we check, with sources

  1. Up to $10,000 toward a new roof built to the IBHS FORTIFIED Roof standard. It pays construction only, and only after the roof is certified. Work done before a grant is not reimbursed.LDI program page; Reg. 126, LAC 37:XIII §18205(D) · Reg. 126 as amended, Louisiana Register Oct. 20, 2024 · source
  2. The home must be in one of the 25 listed parishes (Orleans and Jefferson are on it).LDI program page parish list · page read 2026-10-20; list expanded in the May 27, 2026 release · source
  3. The home must be your primary residence with a homestead exemption. It can't be a condominium or a mobile home.LFHP Homeowner Rules and Requirements · version date 5/29/2026 · source
  4. You need an in-force insurance policy that covers wind, and you upload its declarations page.LFHP Homeowner Rules and Requirements · version date 5/29/2026 · source
  5. If the home is in a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area, you also need a flood insurance policy.LFHP Homeowner Rules and Requirements · version date 5/29/2026 · source
  6. The home must be in good repair, as judged by a FORTIFIED Evaluator, unless the damage is from a hurricane, other wind, or hail.LFHP Homeowner Rules and Requirements · version date 5/29/2026 · source
  7. A home on a dry-stack foundation (bricks or blocks stacked without mortar) needs an approved foundation retrofit before it can qualify.LDI program page and FAQ · read 2026-10-20 · source
  8. Don't hire a contractor or start roof work before LFHP approves the project; doing so makes the home ineligible.LDI FAQ; LFHP Homeowner Rules · FAQ undated, read 2026-10-20 · source
  9. You must disclose every homeowner insurance claim from the past 5 years and any open roof claim.LFHP Homeowner Rules and Requirements · version date 5/29/2026 · source
  10. Selection is by random lottery in announced registration windows, and each round needs a new registration. The 2026 window ran June 1-19, 2026.LDI press release · May 27, 2026 · source
  11. You pay the evaluator, permits, inspections and anything above the grant. LFHP pays the contractor directly after certification.Reg. 126, LAC 37:XIII §18205; Homeowner Rules · Oct. 20, 2024 · source

Does a FORTIFIED roof lower my insurance?

LDI says a FORTIFIED certificate can be shared with your insurer for a discount. Under R.S. 22:1483 insurers give an actuarially justified discount or credit, and LDI says it varies by insurer and can change.
