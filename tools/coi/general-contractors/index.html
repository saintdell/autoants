A routine that takes minutes

  1. When a sub is onboarded, save their certificate PDF in one folder for the job.
  2. Once a month, upload the folder. The sheet sorts by days_to_expiration, so passed and upcoming dates are on top.
  3. Import the calendar once; renewals remind you 30 days ahead.
  4. Ask for the renewal from the sub or their agent when a date comes up. The producer's name is on the row.

What the sheet shows for each sub

  • The general liability, auto, umbrella and workers comp rows the certificate lists, each with its dates.
  • Whether the additional insured and waiver of subrogation boxes are marked on each row, as printed.
  • Who the certificate holder is, so you can see whether it names your company.
  • The limits printed, in whole dollars.

Facts only

Facts as printed on each certificate. A certificate is issued as a matter of information only and confers no rights; it doesn't show whether coverage fits your contract. For that, ask the issuing agent or your own insurance adviser. The converter reports what the certificate prints and when the dates fall. It doesn't judge whether coverage fits a contract, a lease or a law.

Questions

A sub's certificate shows a date that has passed. Does that mean they're uninsured?

Not necessarily: they may have renewed and not sent the new certificate. The sheet only reports the date printed. Ask the sub or their agent for the current certificate.

Can I track one job's subs separately?

Yes: upload each job's certificates as a separate batch, or filter the sheet by certificate_holder or the description of operations.

Does it work for subs' workers comp exemptions?

If the certificate shows no workers comp row, the sheet shows no workers comp row. Any exemption paperwork is a separate document the converter doesn't read.

Will it tell me whether a sub carries enough insurance?

No. It copies what the certificate prints: the limits, the dates, the boxes marked. Comparing those with your contract's terms is your call, or a question for the issuing agent. A certificate confers no rights by itself.

What happens to my files?

They're read in memory and a temporary folder that's deleted as soon as the spreadsheet is made. The spreadsheet and calendar download for 72 hours, then they're deleted too. We keep the order record, not the certificates.
