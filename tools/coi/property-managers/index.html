One list for every property

Upload vendors' certificates as they arrive or in one batch at the start of the year. The sheet lists each vendor's policies with dates and limits; the certificate holder column shows which owner entity or management company each certificate names.

Before a vendor's next visit

  • Sort by expiration to see passed and upcoming dates.
  • Filter by certificate_holder when you manage for several owners.
  • Use the producer column to know which agency to ask for a renewal certificate.

Tenants' certificates too

Commercial tenants who send a liability certificate under their lease can go in the same upload. The sheet records what the certificate prints; the lease decides what it has to show.

Facts only

Facts as printed on each certificate. A certificate is issued as a matter of information only and confers no rights; it doesn't show whether coverage fits your contract. For that, ask the issuing agent or your own insurance adviser. The converter reports what the certificate prints and when the dates fall. It doesn't judge whether coverage fits a contract, a lease or a law.

How a property manager tracks vendor certificate expirations

  1. Collect the certificates. From vendors, contractors and commercial tenants, as PDFs.
  2. Upload by property or all at once. The certificate holder column tells them apart.
  3. Review the dates. Sort by days_to_expiration.
  4. Add the calendar. Import coi-expiry.ics for a reminder 30 days ahead.

Questions

We manage for several owners. Can the sheet separate them?

Yes. The certificate_holder column is the name printed in the holder box, so you can filter by owner entity or by the management company.

A vendor sent a photo of the certificate. Will it read?

Usually. A photo or scan is read with OCR, and every value from it is marked needs_check so you compare it with the picture. A sharp, flat, well-lit page reads best.

Does the calendar include the property address?

It includes the insured's name, the policy type, the policy number and the certificate file name. If the description of operations names the property, that's in the spreadsheet row.

Will it tell me whether a sub carries enough insurance?

No. It copies what the certificate prints: the limits, the dates, the boxes marked. Comparing those with your contract's terms is your call, or a question for the issuing agent. A certificate confers no rights by itself.

What happens to my files?

They're read in memory and a temporary folder that's deleted as soon as the spreadsheet is made. The spreadsheet and calendar download for 72 hours, then they're deleted too. We keep the order record, not the certificates.
