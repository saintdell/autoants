What goes in the record

For every policy on every certificate: who issued it (producer and insurer, with NAIC), the policy number, the effective and expiration dates, the limits printed, and whether ADDL INSD and SUBR WVD are marked. The record also notes how each value was read and its confidence, so a reviewer can see which rows a person checked against the PDF.

A monthly pass

  1. Upload the current certificates for every active sub.
  2. Save the spreadsheet in the job's file with the date you ran it.
  3. Request renewals for dates that have passed or fall within 30 days.
  4. Compare the marked boxes and limits with what your subcontract asks for; that comparison stays with you.

Facts only

Facts as printed on each certificate. A certificate is issued as a matter of information only and confers no rights; it doesn't show whether coverage fits your contract. For that, ask the issuing agent or your own insurance adviser. The converter reports what the certificate prints and when the dates fall. It doesn't judge whether coverage fits a contract, a lease or a law.

Questions

Does the record prove a sub was insured on a given day?

It shows what a certificate printed and when you converted it. A certificate is informational and confers no rights; confirming coverage on a date is a question for the issuing agent or insurer.

Can I keep the original PDFs with the record?

Yes, keep your originals; we delete our copies. The certificate_file column names the PDF each row came from so the two stay linked.

How do I show which rows a person checked?

The needs_check and check_notes columns say which values were read with low confidence. Add your own column for who checked each one and when.

Will it tell me whether a sub carries enough insurance?

No. It copies what the certificate prints: the limits, the dates, the boxes marked. Comparing those with your contract's terms is your call, or a question for the issuing agent. A certificate confers no rights by itself.

What happens to my files?

They're read in memory and a temporary folder that's deleted as soon as the spreadsheet is made. The spreadsheet and calendar download for 72 hours, then they're deleted too. We keep the order record, not the certificates.
