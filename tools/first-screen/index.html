What we look at

  1. Can a customer book from the first screen? A Book or Schedule button they can see without scrolling.
  2. Does it say how soon you can come? Someone with a broken AC wants to know "how soon" before anything else.
  3. Is the online booking you already pay for easy to find? Sometimes it's there, just several screens down.

Most sites land in one of four places:

  • No way to book on the first screenA visitor has to call or dig.
  • Book button, no timeThe button is there, but nothing says how soon.
  • Booking hidden further downYou have online booking, but phone visitors have to scroll to find it.
  • Already says how soonGood. We'll tell you if anything else stands out, or say it looks fine.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No card and no account. If you want help making the change afterwards, we'll talk about that only if you ask.

Do you need my login?

No. We only open your public homepage on a phone-size screen, the same way a customer would.

What if my first screen is already good?

Then we'll say so. Not every site needs a change.

Will you add a "next available" time to my site?

Only if you want it, and only with a time your crew will keep. You set the rule, and when you're full it switches to "Same-day when available".
