    Questions

    Is this the official City fee?

    No. It is an estimate from the City's published schedule. The City's calculation is final, and the Director of Safety & Permits can set the job value higher if it looks low.

    What job value should I use?

    The fair market value of all the work on the permit, materials and labor, including the electrical, gas, mechanical and plumbing equipment, even if you do the work yourself.

    Do I need a permit for a fence or a roof?

    Fences up to 7 feet that meet the zoning rules don't need a building permit. Replacing less than half the roof on a single or double doesn't either. Historic district and Vieux Carré rules can still apply, so check with the HDLC or VCC first.

    What if work starts before the permit?

    The City adds a penalty: 200% of all fees for building work started without a permit, 100% when work starts after you apply but before approval, and five times the listed fee for electrical and mechanical work. This calculator leaves penalties out.

    Who pulls electrical, mechanical and plumbing permits?

    The licensed contractor: a City Class A electrical, A/C or gas contractor, or an Orleans Parish licensed master plumber. Their fee is usually inside their price.
