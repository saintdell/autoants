What the converter reads from Gusto

Gusto's Payroll journal has no labor classification and, in its usual layout, no hours per day. Both are on the WH-347. Put the classification in the workers sheet (or a Classification column), and give daily hours from the Time Tracking Hours report or day columns. If your export splits Social Security and Medicare into separate employee columns, the converter adds them into FICA.

How to export from Gusto and convert to WH-347

  1. Open Payroll journal. In Gusto, go to Reports and choose Payroll journal.
  2. Pick the week. Set the date range to the one pay period that covers the workweek you're reporting.
  3. Add the hour columns. Add regular hours and overtime hours to the report (they're optional columns); keep gross earnings, employee taxes, deductions and net pay.
  4. Download CSV. Generate the report and download it as CSV (Excel works too).
  5. Get hours per day. If you use Gusto Time Tracking, run Reports, then Time Tracking Hours, for the same dates and download it: that's the time report. Without it, add Mon to Sun columns to the export.
  6. Upload both. Upload the Payroll journal, the time report and your workers sheet here, enter the WD number and modification from your contract, and convert.

Menu names are from the provider's help pages as of October 2026 and change over time. If yours differ, look for the report by name.

Questions

My Gusto report shows Social Security and Medicare separately. Where do they go?

Both are FICA on the WH-347 (column 8), so the converter adds the employee Social Security and Medicare columns together. Federal and state withholding go in the tax column.

I pay my crew every two weeks in Gusto. Can I still convert one week?

The WH-347 is weekly. Run the Payroll journal for the pay period and give the converter the daily hours for the week you're reporting; the check flags any row where daily hours don't match the hours paid, so split a biweekly check into its two weeks before you sign.

Do I need Gusto's Plus plan for this?

No. The Payroll journal is enough for rates, gross, deductions and net. The Time Tracking Hours report needs Gusto Time Tracking; without it, add Mon to Sun columns to your export in Excel.

Does the converter sign or submit the payroll?

No. It makes a draft for your review. The contractor checks the Statement of Compliance boxes, signs it and submits the payroll (a sub sends it to the prime). The signature has to be handwritten or a legally valid electronic signature.

What does the rate check compare?

Each worker's cash rate against the WD basic rate, and rate plus fringe credit plus cash in lieu against the WD rate plus fringe for that classification. It also prices overtime hours paid short and hours over 40 paid at straight time, and flags apprentices without registration details. It checks the numbers you gave it against the WD you named; it doesn't decide whether the contract carries Davis-Bacon or which classification a worker actually performed.
