How it works

  1. Export the week from your payroll provider (pick yours below for the exact steps).
  2. Enter the wage determination number and modification from your contract. The converter reads that WD's public text from SAM.gov.
  3. Convert. Full Social Security numbers are cut to the last 4 first; then each worker is matched to a WD classification and checked.
  4. Review the draft, fix anything the rate check found, and sign the Statement of Compliance yourself.

What the rate check looks at

  • Cash rate against the WD basic rate, and rate + fringe against the WD total (fringe can't make up a short basic rate).
  • Overtime: hours over 40 in the workweek at 1.5x the basic rate.
  • Apprentices: registration details, or the full journeyworker rate.
  • Classifications that aren't on the WD (those need a conformance request).
  • Form math: daily hours against hours paid, gross against hours x rates, net against gross minus deductions.

Questions

Does the converter sign or submit the payroll?

No. It makes a draft for your review. The contractor checks the Statement of Compliance boxes, signs it and submits the payroll (a sub sends it to the prime). The signature has to be handwritten or a legally valid electronic signature.

What does the rate check compare?

Each worker's cash rate against the WD basic rate, and rate plus fringe credit plus cash in lieu against the WD rate plus fringe for that classification. It also prices overtime hours paid short and hours over 40 paid at straight time, and flags apprentices without registration details. It checks the numbers you gave it against the WD you named; it doesn't decide whether the contract carries Davis-Bacon or which classification a worker actually performed.

Who needs a WH-347?

Contractors and subcontractors on construction work covered by Davis-Bacon labor standards: direct federal construction, and federally assisted work whose funding brings the standards in. Each one submits a certified payroll for every week of covered work. The WH-347 form itself is optional; the weekly payroll with its content is not.

Where do I find my wage determination number?

In the contract or bid documents: it looks like two letters and eight digits (LA20260041) with a modification number. Use the modification your contract incorporated, not necessarily the newest one.

What does 1 worker-week free mean?

You can convert a whole week; the free download is the complete draft and rate check for the first worker. The summary on screen counts what the check found for everyone. Buy the project-week to download every worker. One free worker-week per visitor every 30 days.
