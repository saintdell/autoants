What the converter reads from ADP

ADP's payroll reports identify workers by File # and don't carry the labor classification or fringe benefit plan details the WH-347 needs. ADP column names differ between RUN, Workforce Now and custom reports; the converter looks for the usual ones below and, if one is missing, names the column it couldn't find so you can rename it.

How to export from ADP and convert to WH-347

  1. Open the payroll reports. In ADP RUN, hover Reports in the main menu, then Payroll. In Workforce Now, go to Reports & Analytics, then All Standard Reports.
  2. Pick the report. RUN: Payroll Summary (hours, wages, taxes and deductions per pay period). Workforce Now: search for Payroll Register.
  3. Set the week. Set the pay period From and To dates for the workweek and leave employees on All, then refresh the report.
  4. Export to Excel. RUN: Export to Excel at the bottom left of the report. Workforce Now: Run As Excel.
  5. Hours per day. Run the Timecard Report for the same dates if you use ADP time, or add Mon to Sun columns to the export.
  6. Upload. Upload the export (.xlsx is fine), the time report and the workers sheet, enter the WD number and modification, and convert.

Menu names are from the provider's help pages as of October 2026 and change over time. If yours differ, look for the report by name.

Questions

ADP shows 'File #' instead of an employee name order I like. Does that matter?

No. File # is used to match each worker to your workers sheet and time report. The WH-347 shows the name and the last 4 digits of an ID; the File # isn't printed on the form.

My ADP export is an .xlsx with a title block above the headers. Will it read?

Delete the title rows so the column headers are on row 1, then upload. The converter reads the first sheet of the workbook.

Is this connected to my ADP account?

No. It's a separate converter from autoants, not affiliated with ADP, and nothing logs in to ADP. It reads the payroll report you export and makes a draft.

Does the converter sign or submit the payroll?

No. It makes a draft for your review. The contractor checks the Statement of Compliance boxes, signs it and submits the payroll (a sub sends it to the prime). The signature has to be handwritten or a legally valid electronic signature.

What does the rate check compare?

Each worker's cash rate against the WD basic rate, and rate plus fringe credit plus cash in lieu against the WD rate plus fringe for that classification. It also prices overtime hours paid short and hours over 40 paid at straight time, and flags apprentices without registration details. It checks the numbers you gave it against the WD you named; it doesn't decide whether the contract carries Davis-Bacon or which classification a worker actually performed.
