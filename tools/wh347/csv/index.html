How to export from CSV or Excel and convert to WH-347

  1. Start from the template. Use the column names in the table below; one row per worker for the week.
  2. Fill the week. Hours each day (Mon to Sun), straight-time and overtime hours, rates, gross, deductions and net pay.
  3. Add the classification. Classification as the wage determination prints it, J or RA, and any fringe credit or cash in lieu per hour.
  4. Save as CSV or .xlsx. Keep the header row on row 1.
  5. Upload. Upload it, enter the WD number and modification from your contract, and convert.

Menu names are from the provider's help pages as of October 2026 and change over time. If yours differ, look for the report by name.

Questions

Which columns are required?

A worker name (or last_name and first_name), straight-time hours (or Mon to Sun) and the straight-time rate. Everything else is optional, but the draft is only as complete as the columns you give it; the review lists what's missing.

Can I upload an Excel file with formulas?

Yes. The converter reads the values Excel last calculated on the first sheet. Save the file in Excel before you upload it so the values are current.

Our timekeeping app exports hours per day. Can I use it?

Yes: upload it as the time report. It needs a worker name or ID, the date and the hours.

Does the converter sign or submit the payroll?

No. It makes a draft for your review. The contractor checks the Statement of Compliance boxes, signs it and submits the payroll (a sub sends it to the prime). The signature has to be handwritten or a legally valid electronic signature.

What does the rate check compare?

Each worker's cash rate against the WD basic rate, and rate plus fringe credit plus cash in lieu against the WD rate plus fringe for that classification. It also prices overtime hours paid short and hours over 40 paid at straight time, and flags apprentices without registration details. It checks the numbers you gave it against the WD you named; it doesn't decide whether the contract carries Davis-Bacon or which classification a worker actually performed.
