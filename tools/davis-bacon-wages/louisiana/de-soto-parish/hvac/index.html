HVAC wage rates in De Soto Parish (Davis-Bacon)

De Soto Parish has 3 hvac rates across 2 construction types. On building work the top hvac base rate is $26.19 plus $14.19 fringe; on residential work the top hvac base rate is $11.77 plus $0.00 fringe.

Rates change, always confirm on SAM.gov. Before you bid or pay, open the official WD: LA20260034 mod 1, LA20260003 mod 1. The WD written into your contract is the one that counts.

    No wage determination lists an "HVAC technician". HVAC work is split by what the worker does: pipe usually falls under plumber or pipefitter, duct under sheet metal worker, and some WDs list unit installation or temperature controls on their own.     What these numbers mean

    Each table is a different construction type, and the type comes from the project, not the trade: an electrician on a school pays the building rate, the same electrician on a sewer plant pays the heavy rate. Base rate plus fringe is the minimum hourly package; the fringe can go into bona fide benefit plans or be paid as cash.

    The rate that binds a job is the one on the WD written into its contract, usually the version in effect when bids opened. Overtime is generally time and a half of the base rate over 40 hours a week; the fringe is not multiplied.

    When did these De Soto Parish rates last change?

    Can the fringe be paid as cash?

    Yes. The fringe can be paid into bona fide benefit plans, as cash on top of the base rate, or a mix, as long as the total meets the base rate plus the fringe.
