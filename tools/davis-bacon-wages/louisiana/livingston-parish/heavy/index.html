Livingston Parish heavy construction wage rates (Davis-Bacon)

Livingston Parish has four heavy wage determinations in effect: LA20260002 (modification 2), LA20260006 (modification 2), LA20260007 (modification 1), LA20260008 (modification 2). Together they list 79 rates for this parish. The highest base rate is $40.34 for Millwright; the lowest is $8.20 for Laborers: Common. The latest change was modification 2, published May 18, 2026.

Rates change, always confirm on SAM.gov. Before you bid or pay, open the official WD: LA20260002 mod 2, LA20260006 mod 2, LA20260007 mod 1, LA20260008 mod 2. The WD written into your contract is the one that counts.

    What these numbers mean

    These are the lowest hourly rates a contractor can pay each trade on a federally funded or federally assisted heavy project in Livingston Parish that carries Davis-Bacon labor standards (generally contracts over $2,000). Each worker gets at least the base rate for the work they do, plus the fringe. The fringe can go into a bona fide benefit plan, be paid as cash on top of the base rate, or a mix of the two.

    43 rates here have a $0.00 fringe. Those come from DOL wage surveys (the identifier starts with SU), so the whole minimum is the base rate. 37 of the 79 rates are union rates and 42 are survey rates.

    The rates don't all date from the same year. The oldest here, Welder (Dredge Under 16") under SULA1994-001, has been in effect since April 1, 1994; the newest since January 1, 2025. Survey rates stay put until DOL runs a new survey, union rates move when the agreement does.

    Heavy work is the catch-all for projects that aren't building, residential or highway: water and sewer lines, flood control, dredging, plants and similar jobs. A parish can have several heavy WDs; the scope line on each one says which jobs it covers.

    Livingston Parish has four heavy WDs. Which one applies depends on the kind of project; the scope line under each WD number above says what it covers.

    7 rates (Carpenter: Including Drywall Hanging/Framing, Metal Studs, and Formsetting/Formbuilding, Ironworker, Ironworker: Structural and Reinforcing, Laborer and others) cover only part of Livingston Parish. The area exactly as the WD prints it is shown under the classification.

    The rates that bind a job are the ones on the WD written into its contract, usually the version in effect when bids opened. Check the WD number and modification in your bid documents before you rely on this table. If a trade you need isn't listed, the contracting agency has to get it added (a conformance request) before you set the pay.

    Overtime on these contracts is generally owed at time and a half of the base rate for hours over 40 in a week. The fringe isn't multiplied. Registered apprentices are paid the rate their program sets.

    The WD also carries the Executive Order 13658 note: on a contract that order covers (generally ones awarded from 2015 to January 29, 2022), workers get at least $13.65 an hour from May 11, 2026 through December 31, 2026, if that's higher than the rate above.

    Questions

    What is the Davis-Bacon rate for a electrician on heavy work in Livingston Parish?

    Electrician: Including Low Voltage Wiring: $29.47 an hour plus $13.50 fringe ($42.97 in all), under LA20260008 modification 2, in effect since January 1, 2025. 2 other electrician rates are listed too. Confirm on SAM.gov before you bid or pay.

    When did these Livingston Parish rates last change?

    The latest change on this page is LA20260002 modification 2, published May 18, 2026. DOL publishes modifications on SAM.gov; we check weekly.

    Can the fringe be paid as cash?

    Yes. The fringe can be paid into bona fide benefit plans, as cash on top of the base rate, or a mix, as long as the total meets the base rate plus the fringe.
