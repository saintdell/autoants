    What these numbers mean

    These are the lowest hourly rates a contractor can pay each trade on a federally funded or federally assisted residential project in St Tammany Parish that carries Davis-Bacon labor standards (generally contracts over $2,000). Each worker gets at least the base rate for the work they do, plus the fringe. The fringe can go into a bona fide benefit plan, be paid as cash on top of the base rate, or a mix of the two.

    3 rates here have a $0.00 fringe. Those come from DOL wage surveys (the identifier starts with SU), so the whole minimum is the base rate. 3 of the 9 rates are union rates and 6 are survey rates.

    The rates don't all date from the same year. The oldest here, Roofer under SULA2004-012, has been in effect since June 15, 2004; the newest since May 26, 2025. Survey rates stay put until DOL runs a new survey, union rates move when the agreement does.

    Residential work means single-family homes and apartment buildings up to four stories.

    1 rate (Plumber: Including HVAC Pipe) covers only part of St Tammany Parish. The area exactly as the WD prints it is shown under the classification.

    The rates that bind a job are the ones on the WD written into its contract, usually the version in effect when bids opened. Check the WD number and modification in your bid documents before you rely on this table. If a trade you need isn't listed, the contracting agency has to get it added (a conformance request) before you set the pay.

    Overtime on these contracts is generally owed at time and a half of the base rate for hours over 40 in a week. The fringe isn't multiplied. Registered apprentices are paid the rate their program sets.

    The WD also carries the Executive Order 13658 note: on a contract that order covers (generally ones awarded from 2015 to January 29, 2022), workers get at least $13.65 an hour from May 11, 2026 through December 31, 2026, if that's higher than the rate above.

    Questions

    What is the Davis-Bacon rate for a electrician on residential work in St Tammany Parish?

    Electrician: Including Low Voltage Wiring and Installation of Fire Alarms and Security Systems: $30.35 an hour plus $12.46 fringe ($42.81 in all), under LA20260001 modification 2, in effect since May 26, 2025. Confirm on SAM.gov before you bid or pay.

    Which wage determination covers residential construction in St Tammany Parish?

    LA20260001 (modification 2, published May 18, 2026).

    When did these St Tammany Parish rates last change?

    The latest change on this page is LA20260001 modification 2, published May 18, 2026. DOL publishes modifications on SAM.gov; we check weekly.

    Can the fringe be paid as cash?

    Yes. The fringe can be paid into bona fide benefit plans, as cash on top of the base rate, or a mix, as long as the total meets the base rate plus the fringe.
