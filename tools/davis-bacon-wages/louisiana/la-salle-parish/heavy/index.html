La Salle Parish heavy construction wage rates (Davis-Bacon)

La Salle Parish has three heavy wage determinations in effect: LA20260004 (modification 2), LA20260006 (modification 2), LA20260007 (modification 1). Together they list 67 rates for this parish. The highest base rate is $40.34 for Millwright; the lowest is $7.60 for Laborers: (Common). The latest change was modification 2, published May 18, 2026.

Rates change, always confirm on SAM.gov. Before you bid or pay, open the official WD: LA20260004 mod 2, LA20260006 mod 2, LA20260007 mod 1. The WD written into your contract is the one that counts.

    What these numbers mean

    These are the lowest hourly rates a contractor can pay each trade on a federally funded or federally assisted heavy project in La Salle Parish that carries Davis-Bacon labor standards (generally contracts over $2,000). Each worker gets at least the base rate for the work they do, plus the fringe. The fringe can go into a bona fide benefit plan, be paid as cash on top of the base rate, or a mix of the two.

    45 rates here have a $0.00 fringe. Those come from DOL wage surveys (the identifier starts with SU), so the whole minimum is the base rate. 26 of the 67 rates are union rates and 41 are survey rates.

    The rates don't all date from the same year. The oldest here, Welder (Dredge Under 16") under SULA1994-001, has been in effect since April 1, 1994; the newest since September 1, 2025. Survey rates stay put until DOL runs a new survey, union rates move when the agreement does.

    Heavy work is the catch-all for projects that aren't building, residential or highway: water and sewer lines, flood control, dredging, plants and similar jobs. A parish can have several heavy WDs; the scope line on each one says which jobs it covers.

    La Salle Parish has three heavy WDs. Which one applies depends on the kind of project; the scope line under each WD number above says what it covers.

    5 rates (Electrician, Ironworker, Plumber and Steamfitter, Roofer) cover only part of La Salle Parish. The area exactly as the WD prints it is shown under the classification.

    The rates that bind a job are the ones on the WD written into its contract, usually the version in effect when bids opened. Check the WD number and modification in your bid documents before you rely on this table. If a trade you need isn't listed, the contracting agency has to get it added (a conformance request) before you set the pay.

    Overtime on these contracts is generally owed at time and a half of the base rate for hours over 40 in a week. The fringe isn't multiplied. Registered apprentices are paid the rate their program sets.

    The WD also carries the Executive Order 13658 note: on a contract that order covers (generally ones awarded from 2015 to January 29, 2022), workers get at least $13.65 an hour from May 11, 2026 through December 31, 2026, if that's higher than the rate above.

    When did these La Salle Parish rates last change?

    Can the fringe be paid as cash?

    Yes. The fringe can be paid into bona fide benefit plans, as cash on top of the base rate, or a mix, as long as the total meets the base rate plus the fringe.
