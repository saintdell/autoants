Starting a business in Metairie?

Metairie is unincorporated Jefferson Parish, so there's no city license. Get a zoning clearance first, then file the General Registration Application with the Jefferson Parish Sheriff's Office Bureau of Revenue and Taxation. It also covers parish sales tax, and the minimum is $50 per location. Our free checklist has every official link.
