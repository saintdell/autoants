Starting a business in New Orleans?

Every business in Orleans Parish needs an occupational license. Confirm the zoning allows the use and the site has a Certificate of Occupancy, apply on the City's One Stop site, and send the Amended Return to the City's Bureau of Revenue within your first 40 days of operating.
