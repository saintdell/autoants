## Questions

### Will you get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can promise that. We fix what Google and AI answers read, then count calls, booked jobs and how often you're named, before and after.

### Do I keep my Google profile?

Yes. You stay the Primary Owner. We're added as a Manager, which can't remove you or delete the profile, and we never ask for your password.

### How is this different from SEO I've paid for before?

You see the work as counts you can check: listings fixed, pages added, how many AI answers named you, and the calls that came in. No 50-keyword ranking report.

No. Real local links only: chambers, associations, partners and local news. No link packages.

No. We ask every customer, the same way every time. Asking only happy customers is review gating, and Google bans it.
