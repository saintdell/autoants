autoants, the facts

The short version, kept in one place. Checked 2026-11-18.

Name

autoants, short for "automated assistants". Legal name: AutoAnts, LLC.

Founder

Lane Davis II. He runs autoants himself, and owners deal with him directly.

What we do

autoants answers the calls a local business misses, follows up the quotes it forgets, and helps customers find it on Google and in AI answers, so more of the work it already pays for turns into booked jobs. It starts with a free breakdown of the business, made from public information, that shows which fix the owner's own numbers need.

Who we help

  • Home services and trades (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, tree, landscaping): the core focus
  • Also auto shops, salons and nail spas, gyms and studios, pet businesses, restaurants and event businesses, and professional services (accountants, insurance, real estate, tutors)
  • Dental and medical offices: not yet; patient information needs a HIPAA program first

Every kind of business we help.

Service area

Louisiana: Greater New Orleans, Baton Rouge and the Northshore first. Towns we serve: New Orleans, Metairie, Kenner, Harahan, River Ridge, Gretna, Harvey, Marrero, Chalmette, LaPlace, Covington, Mandeville, Slidell, Hammond, Baton Rouge, Prairieville, Gonzales, Denham Springs.

autoants comes to you or meets by phone and video; there is no storefront, so no street address is listed.

Prices, in short

  • Get Found and catch missed calls: $1,250 a month + $1,250 setup. Plus a monthly market pack: permit share, jobs coming due, neighborhood pages, competitor watch.
  • Not ready? Try one: $500 once. One assistant on the owner's phone. Most shops start with texting back every missed call; others pick web-form answers, booking and reminders, the review ask, service-due or invoice reminders, or a "book now" box on the website. The rules we keep

  • We don't promise calls, rankings or jobs. We measure where a business is now and show the same numbers later.
  • Every AI that talks to customers says it's an AI.
  • Nothing goes to a customer in a new form until the owner has approved the wording.
  • Texts go only to people who contacted the business first or said yes, and every text honors STOP.
  • No paid link placements, no fake or bought reviews, no seeded or undisclosed mentions.
  • The business owns its accounts, pages, listings and customer list.
  • No patient information: dental and medical offices wait until there's a HIPAA program.

What each assistant will never do: autoants.com/never.

  • autoants doesn't serve dental or medical offices with patient information yet.
