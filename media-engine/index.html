Every week

What's in a week.

  • Posts from your jobs. A before/after clip from your photos, 2 Google posts, 2 Facebook or Instagram posts, 1 Nextdoor post, and 1 review highlight.
  • Data posts about your ZIPs. A carousel and a stat post from the city's permit records, each with its source and dates.
  • Captions and a short-video script. Captions written for Facebook, Instagram, Google and Nextdoor.
  • Weather posts. When the Weather Service issues an alert your trade watches for, a ready post and a note to your customers.
  • A monthly recap video. 60 seconds on the month.

Plain rules

What we don't promise.

No promised results

We don't promise calls, jobs, rankings or revenue. We promise true posts, on time, for your OK.

No borrowed numbers

Every number is yours or the city's, with its source and date. No industry statistics from somewhere else.

No one's address

Permit counts are by ZIP. No homeowner names or addresses, ever.

Nothing without your yes

We don't post on our own, and we don't invent customers or reviews.
