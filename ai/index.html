What AutoAnts is

AutoAnts is the AI services wing of Blundell Core Holdings, founded and run by Lane Davis, a licensed Louisiana attorney. It builds websites, after-hours lead-intake systems, and AI-search visibility for Louisiana's blue-collar service businesses — tree services, pressure washing, concrete, and other trades. Its signature model: the first deliverable is free. AutoAnts builds a working preview website for a business before any money changes hands.
