What AutoAnts is

AutoAnts (the name is short for "automated assistants") is a Louisiana company founded and run by Lane Davis II. It builds websites, after-hours lead-intake systems, and AI-search visibility for Louisiana's local businesses: trades, auto shops, salons, gyms and more. Its signature model: the first deliverable is free. AutoAnts makes a company breakdown of a business from public information before any money changes hands.

Who it is for

  • Louisiana service businesses with no website, a broken website, or a site that can't capture after-hours inquiries
  • Owner-operators who miss storm-season and after-hours calls because they're on a job — and lose the work to whoever answers first
  • Local businesses that don't appear when customers ask AI assistants for recommendations, and want the legitimate signals fixed

The assistants

Each automated assistant ("Ant") does one job. The owner approves anything that goes to a customer.

  • Text-Back Ant: texts back a missed call within seconds
  • Responder Ant: answers a new web or social lead within seconds
  • Scheduler Ant: turns quote requests into estimates and booked appointments
  • Reviewer Ant: asks every customer for a review after the job, the same way every time
  • Receptionist Ant: an AI that answers calls the owner can't pick up, says up front that it's an AI, and texts the details to the owner
  • Scout Ant: local listings and AI-search visibility
  • Reminder Ant: reaches past customers who agreed to hear from the business
  • Promoter Ant: short job videos and local ads
