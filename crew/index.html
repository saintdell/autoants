Upkeepi Crew

Property-care jobs near you. Take the ones you want.

Upkeepi offers lawn, cleanup, pressure-washing, gutter and cleaning jobs to local crews at a set price. The first to accept gets the job. You're paid per job.

How it works

  • You choose every job.An offer shows the work, the area, the time window and the pay before you decide. Skipping a job, or not answering, never counts against you.
  • You set your own availability and area.Change them any time. We never schedule you.
  • Your tools, your methods.We describe the result and the before-and-after photos we need. How you get there is up to you.
  • Work for anyone else.No exclusivity, no minimums, no quotas.
  • Paid per job, not by the hour.Pay is released when the job's photos are approved, through Stripe. Stripe collects your tax details; we never ask for your Social Security number.
  • Rush and storm work pays more, and the extra is shown on its own line in the offer.

Insurance and checks

After you apply, we'll ask for your certificate of insurance by email. Most services need at least $300,000 per occurrence; pressure washing, gutters, debris and treatment need $500,000; tree work needs $1,000,000.

Services inside homes need a background check through a consumer reporting agency. If you pick one, we'll send a separate disclosure and authorization form. Nothing is ordered without your written OK, and we keep only the result status, never the report.

How we reach you

Job offers come by email. Texts are optional.

What happens next

  1. We email you the crew agreement to read, and ask for your insurance certificate.
  2. You set up payouts with Stripe (Stripe asks for your tax details, not us).
  3. Offers start arriving for the services, areas and times you picked. Take any, skip any.
