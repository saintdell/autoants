Pay an invoice or quote

Custom work, bigger projects and anything we quoted for you by hand gets its own invoice. You pay it on a page Stripe hosts, not on this one.

  1. Find our emailThe invoice comes from Stripe on behalf of AutoAnts, LLC, with the amount, what it covers and the due date.
  2. Open "Pay this invoice"The page that opens is on invoice.stripe.com. Pay by card, or by bank transfer if the invoice offers it.
  3. Get your receiptStripe emails a receipt the moment it goes through. Keep it for your books.
Can't find it, or the amount looks wrong? Don't pay it. Email hello@autoants.com and we'll resend or fix it. We never ask for card numbers by email, text or phone.
