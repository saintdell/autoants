How Verified Pros works

A contractor is listed only when it passes all of these, checked on the date each page shows:

  1. An active, unexpired LSLBC license certificate in a class for the trade (roofing: Roofing and Sheet Metal, Siding, Residential Roofing or Residential Construction; plumbing: Plumbing; HVAC: Mechanical or Heat, Air Conditioning, Ventilation Duct Work and Refrigeration). No license on the record suspended or revoked. A home improvement registration doesn't count.
  2. General liability insurance on file with the LSLBC, not expired. The page shows the month it expires.
  3. At least one permit in that trade credited to the business name in that neighborhood in the last 24 full months, from the City of New Orleans' open permit records.
  4. A license record read within 45 days.
  5. Not on our removal list.

Order. Listed A to Z by business name. The order says nothing about quality, and paying partners never get a better place.

Money. Some listed contractors pay Lane Davis II a referral fee when he introduces a customer; that never affects who's listed or the order. A referral partner's entry says so on the entry itself. Nobody pays to be listed or to move up.

Never shown. Homeowner or job addresses (no street address at all), permit numbers, ratings, reviews or any ranking by quality.

Sources. The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors' public license records (license number, type, class, status, first-issued date, insurance on file, business phone); the City of New Orleans' open permit records (Permits - BLDS, data.nola.gov 72f9-bi28), placed in the City's Neighborhood Statistical Areas; and a business's own website, listed only when its phone number appears on it. Plumbers in Louisiana are licensed by the State Plumbing Board, whose roster we can't read automatically, so the plumbing pages use the LSLBC's commercial Plumbing class. Verify any license at lslbc.louisiana.gov.

Refresh. At least monthly, after the City's permit data and the license records update. Each page shows the day it was last refreshed.

Correct or remove a listing

Email hello@autoants.com. Put 'Correct' or 'Remove' and the business name in the subject, and include the LSLBC license number. To correct a fact, say which one and link the public record or the page on the business's own site that shows it. To be removed, just say so: the business comes off every Verified Pros page and the New Orleans guide at the next rebuild and stays off, even though the records are public. Both are free.
