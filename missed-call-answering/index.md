## What happens when a call rings out

1. You keep your number. Calls you don't answer forward to the assistant, and you can turn forwarding off yourself.
2. The caller gets one text back that names your business and lets them reply STOP. A landline can't receive it.
3. Their reply comes to you, tagged, so you decide what happens next.
4. Add the AI receptionist and a voice answers instead. It says it's an AI in its first sentence and texts you the job.

## What it won't do

- Quote a price, promise a time or give out an address.
- Text anyone who didn't contact you first.
- Send a new kind of message before you've approved the wording.
- Stand in for you when someone wants a person: they get passed to you.

## Questions

### Is this an answering service?

No. An answering service puts a person on your calls. autoants texts back the calls you miss, follows up your quotes and books appointments. A voice answer is the AI receptionist, and it always says it's an AI.

### Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and customers keep calling it. Calls you don't answer forward to the assistant, your number is never moved, and you can turn forwarding off yourself.

### When can the texts start?

Business texts have to be registered with the phone carriers first, under your business's name. We file the registration the day you say yes and tell you the go-live date in writing.

### Will Google's AI call my business for customers?

Not in Louisiana. Google's help page lists Louisiana as one of five states where its automated calls to businesses aren't available (checked November 18, 2026). The calls that count here come from people.

### Do you promise more jobs?

No. We count your missed calls and booked jobs for one real week before we start, then show you the same numbers every month.

### Who owns the number and the customer list?

You do. Texting runs on an account in your business's name, and your customer list, pages and listings stay yours if you leave.
