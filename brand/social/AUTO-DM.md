# Auto-DM for @autoants (comment PREVIEW, get the link)

Our own comment-to-DM, and the 30-day proof run before we sell it to clients as Comment-to-Quote (tree-lead-engine `products/comment-to-quote/`).

## What happens
1. **The post or reel says:** "Want to see your new website before you pay a dime? Comment PREVIEW."
2. **Someone comments PREVIEW** (any case, anywhere in the comment).
3. **They get one DM:**
   > Hey {first_name}! Thanks for the comment. Here's how to get a free preview of your new website, built before you pay anything: https://autoants.com/?ref=dm#contact
   > This is an automatic reply from autoants. Lane will follow up personally.
4. **Optional public reply** to their comment, rotating between "Sent you a DM!", "Check your messages 🐜" and "Link's in your DMs." The rotation keeps it from looking like a bot wall.
5. **If they write back,** you answer within 24 hours. Instagram only allows follow-up messages inside that window.

## Setup (about 20 minutes, your logins, $0)
1. **Switch Instagram @autoants to a professional account:** Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account → Business → category "Software company" (not anything legal).
2. **Connect it to the Facebook page** (facebook.com/autoantshq) when Instagram offers.
3. **Create a ManyChat account** at manychat.com on the **Free** plan: 25 active contacts a month, plenty at launch. Connect Instagram, and Facebook if you want comment replies there too.
4. **Automation → New → "User comments on your Post or Reel":**
   - posts: any post or reel;
   - keyword: "contains PREVIEW";
   - public reply: on, with the three rotating lines above;
   - DM: the message above, including the "automatic reply" line.
5. **Test it:** comment PREVIEW from your personal account. The DM should arrive in seconds, and the link should open the site's contact form.
6. **Tell me it's live.** I'll switch the post captions to "Comment PREVIEW" and set a 30-day check-in to read the numbers.

## Rules
- **Only people who commented PREVIEW** ever get a DM. No mass DMs and no auto-follows.
- **The DM always says it's automatic.**
- **One message, then you.** No drip sequence.
- **Never ask for a follow before sending the link.** Some tools offer that "follow gate"; leave it off.
- **Upgrade only past 25 contacts a month.** Essential is about $17/mo (checked 2026-10-01). Your card, your call.

## The 30-day proof (what we'll measure)
- PREVIEW comments per post;
- DMs delivered;
- link clicks: `ref=dm` visits to the contact section;
- preview requests that came from a DM.

After 30 days, those numbers become the case for selling Comment-to-Quote to clients, or the reason not to.
