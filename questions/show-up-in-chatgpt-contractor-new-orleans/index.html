What the answers read

  • Your own site: pages for each service and town, with plain answers to the questions customers ask.
  • Your Google profile: categories, hours, services and photos that match the site.
  • Listings and lists: the directories and local roundups the answers cite, with your name, address and phone the same everywhere.
  • Reviews: real ones, asked of every customer.

What autoants does, and won't do

  • Asks the same questions 3 times each and logs every answer, because answers change run to run.
  • Fixes only real signals: complete profiles, consistent listings, structured data, real reviews.
  • No paid placements, no seeded or undisclosed mentions, no fake reviews.
  • Every number is measured, not promised.
