Why HVAC shops ask for it first

The no-cool call in July goes to whoever answers. A tech on a roof can't, so the text holds the caller while you finish. It doesn't replace answering the phone; if you want calls answered too, that's the receptionist, an AI that says it's an AI.

What a text-back needs

Business texts in the US go through carrier registration (10DLC), and marketing texts need written consent. A text-back answers someone who just called you, which is different from a blast. At autoants the number and texts run on your own Twilio account.

What to ask any provider

  1. How fast does the text go out, and what does it say?
  2. Does it come from my own business number, and who owns the number and the accounts if I leave?
  3. What exactly does it say to a customer, and do I approve the wording first?
  4. What's the setup fee, the monthly fee and the minimum commitment, in writing?
