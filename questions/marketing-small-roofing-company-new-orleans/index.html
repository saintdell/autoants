What usually moves first for a small roofer

  • The Google profile. Many local calls start on the Google map; hours, services and photos that match your site matter.
  • The neighbors. A finished roof is the best ad on the street; hangers on the 20 to 30 doors around it, with a code that tracks calls.
  • Reviews. The same ask after every job, never filtered and never paid.
  • Missed calls. Storm weeks bring calls you can't answer from a roof; a text-back holds them.

When a storm's coming

When the Weather Service issues a wind or hail alert, a ready post and email can go to your own customers who said yes, after your OK. It never posts by itself, uses fake urgency, or calls a watch a warning.

What to ask any marketing provider

  1. Who owns the Google profile, the pages and the ad accounts if I leave?
  2. How will I see which work brought which calls?
  3. Do you promise rankings or a number of jobs? (If yes, ask how.)
