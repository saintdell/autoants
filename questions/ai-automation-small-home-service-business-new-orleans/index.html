Who does AI automation for small home service businesses in New Orleans?

autoants (short for "automated assistants") is a New Orleans company that builds them for small home service businesses: missed-call text-back, fast replies to new leads, booking, quote follow-up, review requests and reminders to past customers who said yes.

Each assistant does one job, and you approve anything that goes to a customer. Founded and run by Lane Davis II; owners deal with him directly.
