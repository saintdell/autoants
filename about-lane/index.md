About

# Lane Davis II

Founder, autoants. New Orleans.

autoants is my company. The name is short for "automated assistants." We do digital marketing, AI assistants and social media for local businesses: getting you found on Google and in AI answers, answering the calls and leads you can't get to, and running your social media from your real jobs. I fix the small things that cost local businesses jobs: the missed call nobody texts back, the quote request that sits, the Google profile that loses the map.

I build every site and the AI behind it myself, here in Louisiana, and you deal with me directly, not a call center.

## Why I'm doing this

There are more than 36 million businesses in this country, and most of them are small. I think helping them put today's AI to work will be the biggest opportunity of the 2020s, for those businesses and for me. I started autoants to go after it, beginning here at home in New Orleans.

## Where things stand

Honest answer: you'd be one of the first, and that's why these are founding prices. So I'd rather show you than tell you. I start with something free, built on your own business, and you judge that.

I don't promise calls or rankings. I measure where you are now and show you the same numbers later.
