Who’s a great referral

Owners of home-service shops: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, pest control, pool and tree service. Usually a small crew, where the owner is on the job and the phone rings in the truck.

Listen for one phrase:

“We miss calls when we’re out on a job.”

When you hear it, that’s the intro.
