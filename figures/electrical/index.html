How your breaker panel works, and what to check when an outlet goes dead

Every outlet and light in the house runs through one breaker in this panel. Here's one circuit with too much on it.

Tap Turn on the heater, then Add the hair dryer, then Reset.

The breaker tripped. Too much on one circuit. Unplug one thing, push the handle all the way to OFF, then back to ON. If it trips again, call an electrician.

Why does a breaker trip?

To protect the wiring. When a circuit carries more than it's made for, or there's a fault, the breaker cuts it off and its handle stops in the middle.

What can I check myself?

Unplug something on that circuit, then reset the breaker once: OFF, then ON. Don't take the inner cover off the panel. If it trips again, or you see scorch marks or smell burning, call an electrician.

What does an electrician check first?

What's on that circuit and whether it's too much for it, then the breaker itself, the wiring and the outlets on that circuit.
