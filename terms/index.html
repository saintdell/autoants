The autoants service agreement

Plain English. This is the agreement you sign when you start; the order page fills in your business and the services you chose.

AutoAnts LLC ("autoants"), PO Box 172, Arabi, LA 70032, and [Your business's legal name] ("you") agree:

1. What autoants does. The services checked below, as described on the "Your Plan" page that comes with this agreement and is part of it:

  • Try one: one assistant ($500 once, founding rate; credited toward setup if you start a monthly plan within 30 days)
  • Catch missed calls ($750 setup + $750/month, founding rate)
  • Catch missed calls, pay per job ($750 setup + $250/month + $25 per recovered job, never more than $750/month, founding rate)
  • Get Found: your website rebuilt (up to 10 pages) and ongoing local SEO (the monthly work listed on the "Your Plan" page), $500 setup + $750/month
  • Get Found for a franchise location: your Google profile, listings and the business facts AI assistants read about you kept current, with new pages only where your franchise allows (the monthly work listed on the "Your Plan" page), $500 setup + $750/month
  • Google ads add-on: your Google Local Services Ads and Search ads managed in your own Google accounts, on top of Get Found or Catch missed calls ($500 setup + $500/month; you pay Google for the ads directly)
  • Get Found and catch missed calls ($1,250 setup + $1,250/month, founding rate)
  • Add-on: Get the next job, on top of both ($1,750 setup + $2,500/month in all, founding rate)
  • New website: up to 10 pages, yours to keep ($1,000 once; free with Get Found and catch missed calls)
  • Site Care: hosting and upkeep for a website we built ($100/month)
  • AI Opportunity Assessment ($500, credited toward setup if you continue within 60 days; any part the setup fee doesn't use comes off your first month)
  • Operations Math monthly plan (priced by size, see Schedule A)
  • Other: ________________

The prices above are for one location with up to 15 trucks or crews (or the same size in your field). Bigger operations are quoted in writing after we look at your numbers.

2. Payment. Setup fees are due when you say go. If you bought Try one and start a monthly plan within 30 days of paying for it, its price is credited toward that plan's setup fee. If you prepay twelve months of a monthly plan, its setup fee is waived. If you cancel before we start the build, we refund the setup fee in full. The setup fee pays for work we start right away, so it isn't refundable once the build begins. If we can't finish the setup for reasons within our control, we refund the part of the setup fee for work not done. Your first month is charged with your setup fee, and each month after on the same date. If a payment fails, we'll let you know and retry it over 7 days; if it's still unpaid after that, we pause the service until it's paid. If it's still unpaid 30 days after it was due, we can end the service. If a charge looks wrong, tell us before disputing it with your card company, and we'll sort it out. Founding rates stay the same for your first 12 months of service; after that, we may change the price with at least 30 days' written notice. If you're on pay per job, a recovered job is a job booked from a call you missed that our assistant answered or texted back, listed on your monthly report; you have 30 days to question any of them, and the $250 base plus job charges never come to more than $750 a month.

3. Getting started. Once you say go and give us what section 5 lists, we start the work and tell you the go-live date in writing.

4. Term and cancelling. Monthly services renew each month until you or we cancel. The first 90 days start on the date of your first monthly charge. If you cancel during them, the months left in those 90 days are still due, unless we raise your price, which lets you cancel without that minimum. After them, you can cancel by email to lane@autoants.com, effective at the end of the month you've paid for. We can end the service with 30 days' notice; if we do, and you haven't broken this agreement, we refund any part of a month you've paid for and won't get. Either of us can end this agreement early if the other seriously breaks it and doesn't fix the problem within 15 days of written notice. If we can't deliver a main part of your service for 30 days for reasons within our control, you can cancel and get back what you prepaid for it.

5. What you provide. Accurate business information. Your accounts stay yours: you own them and log in to them yourself, and you add us as a user or manager where we need access. We never ask for or hold your passwords, and you can remove our access at any time. When we ask you to approve something, a quick reply keeps things moving; anything waiting on your OK waits, and the monthly fee stays the same. You confirm you have the right to use the photos and content you give us. If someone claims something you gave us violates their rights, or that a contact list you gave us lacked the permission the law requires for texts or calls, you'll handle that claim, as long as we tell you promptly; this doesn't cover changes we made without your OK or material we created. If someone claims material we created for you violates their rights, we'll handle that claim the same way.

5a. Franchise locations. If your business is a franchise, you'll show us the franchise's marketing rules that apply to the work, and we'll work inside them. Approvals your franchisor requires are yours to get; we'll prepare what you need to ask for them. Before work starts, we'll list what your franchisor controls and what needs its approval. Where the rules stop part of the monthly work (for example new website pages), we'll agree in writing on replacement work of similar value; if we can't agree within 30 days, either of us can drop that part and the monthly fee goes down to match.

6. Your approval. Nothing is sent to your customers in a new form until you've approved the wording. You can change or pause any assistant at any time.

7. Texts and calls. Before any text or outbound call goes out for you, we agree on what it's for and who keeps proof of the permission the law requires. A reply to someone who contacted you first is fine; a marketing text, or a marketing call with an AI voice, goes only to people who gave written permission first. You confirm you have that permission for any list you give us. Every text names your business and lets them reply STOP, and we honor any other clear request to stop, in any form, along with quiet hours and do-not-call rules. Phone assistants say up front that they're AI, and texts from an assistant say they're automated. An assistant can get an answer wrong, and any price it gives is an estimate unless you've told us it's firm. The assistants aren't an emergency line: a caller who describes an emergency, like a gas smell, a fire or flooding, is told to hang up and call 911. Text messaging requires registering your business with the carriers; you'll provide the information needed for it.

7a. Google ads. When the Google ads add-on is checked: the Google Ads and Local Services Ads accounts are yours, in your business's name, and you pay Google for the ads directly on your own card. We're added as a user or a linked manager, and our access ends when the service ends. You set the budget; we suggest one, and any change to it waits for your OK. Our setup fee and our monthly management fee are the flat amounts in section 1, separate from what Google charges you and never a percentage of your ad spend; they're shown in writing before your first ad spend and on every invoice we send. Each month we send a report that shows what Google charged you, with clicks and impressions, apart from our fees, and you can always sign in to your own accounts. We also send you Google's guide to working with third parties. We review Local Services leads with you and submit the disputes Google's current rules allow; Google decides each one. Your Google Business Profile itself is free from Google. You're responsible for the license and insurance details you give Google for its ads. Google sets its own rules and prices, can reject an ad or pause an account, and decides where ads show; we can't guarantee a number of leads, a cost per lead or a position.

8. Your data and your site. Your customer information and your website content belong to you. We use them only to provide the services and never sell them (section 11). When the service ends, we delete them within 60 days without your needing to ask (section 11.7). You own your domain, your business phone number, your Google profile, your listings and your ad accounts. If you leave, we give you an export of your content and customer data and help move your site, number and account access to you or your next provider; you can ask for that export any time within 30 days after the service ends. When you ask, we hand full ownership of your Google Business Profile back to you within 7 business days, and any business phone number we set up for you moves to you or your next provider on request. "Your Plan" lists anything that can't transfer (for example a tool licensed to us).

9. Results. We'll do the work described and report the numbers honestly, but we can't guarantee a specific number of calls, jobs, reviews or rankings.

10. Using your name. We name you as a client or share your results only as section 11.4 allows.

10a. Credit to autoants. Work we make for you carries a small credit to autoants: a "Website by autoants" link in your website's footer, an @autoants tag on social posts we make, and "Made with autoants" in small type on the last slide or end card of videos. Never on cover images or your main photos, and never worded as autoants endorsing your business. A case study about you on autoants' own site or posts follows sections 10b and 11.4.

10b. Case study. As a founding client, you agree that autoants may publish one case study about your results with your business name. If your franchise agreement requires the franchisor's OK to name your business, the case study waits for it. You'll see it before it's published and can correct anything inaccurate or remove anything private, such as customer details or revenue you don't want shown.

11. Data, publicity and public records.

11.1 Your data. We use your data, including your customers' information, only to deliver the services. We may also combine figures from many clients into de-identified, aggregated data (for example benchmarks and market reports) that never identifies you or any of your customers and always draws on at least 5 businesses. We own those aggregate outputs.

11.1a Call records. When a phone assistant answers for you, it tells the caller the call is recorded. Our phone service (Vapi, listed in 11.6) holds the recording and transcript. We also keep our own record of each call (time, length, outcome, and the words with your customers' names, numbers, emails and addresses removed). We use these to check and improve the assistant's answers and to give you a monthly report. We don't keep call records for health-care clients, and no patient health information goes through our assistants unless we've signed a separate agreement for it (a BAA). All of these are deleted with the rest of your data under 11.7.

11.2 De-identified data. We may use de-identified totals drawn from at least 5 businesses, never naming you, your business or your customers, in reports and data products. Data that identifies you or your customers is shared only if you opt in under a separate written agreement; otherwise we never sell or share it, except with the services in 11.6 that help us deliver your service.

11.3 Licensing your data (brokerage). If you choose to license data you own to a buyer (for example an AI company), that's a separate written agreement for each deal. You approve each deal before anything is shared, our fee is disclosed in writing, and we show you what you net after it.

11.4 Publicity. (a) Your marketing: in the marketing we make for you, we may use your name, logo, job photos and results. (b) Our marketing: our case studies, posts and website use your name, results or photos only with your OK on each piece, apart from the one case study in section 10b. (c) Testimonials: only with your consent, in your own words. (d) Your customers: we don't show their faces or names without their OK, and we leave out house numbers, street signs and addresses. (e) Client list: we may list your business name among our clients. Tell us any time and we'll take it off.

11.5 Public-records products (Next Job Mailer, the parcel map, the data Ants). These are built from publicly available records. We're not a consumer reporting agency, and you may not use anything we give you to decide whether anyone is eligible for credit, insurance, employment or housing (Fair Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. §1681 et seq.). You won't use it to target, exclude or treat anyone differently because of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin or any other class the law protects (federal Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. §3601 et seq.; Louisiana Equal Housing Opportunity Act, La. R.S. 51:2601 et seq.). Anyone who asks not to be mailed is left off every client's mailings.

11.6 Services that handle data for us: Anthropic (AI assistant), Twilio (texts and calls), PostGrid or Lob (printing and mailing), Cloudflare (forms, hosting and storage), Google Workspace (email and files), OpenAI (a safety check on AI receptionist replies), Vapi (AI receptionist calls), Stripe (payments), DocuSeal (signing agreements), Fathom (recording calls with you, only with your OK), Plaud (recording and transcribing meetings with you, only with your OK), Telegram (call summaries to our phone) and the US Census geocoder (matching addresses). We may update this list; we'll tell you at least 14 days before a new one handles your data. If you object, tell us within those 14 days; if we can't serve you without it, you may cancel without the 90-day minimum applying. We also use other AI tools (ChatGPT, Grok and Meta's Muse) for research and drafts, but never with your customers' information or anything you've shared with us privately.

11.7 Keeping and deleting. Within 60 days after the service ends, we delete your customers' information and your business information from the systems we control and ask the services in 11.6 to delete theirs, or hand it back if you ask first. We keep only billing records, this agreement, anything you approved for publishing (like the case study), the de-identified totals in 11.2, the minimum needed to keep honoring opt-outs (a STOP or do-not-mail entry), and what the law requires.

12. Limits. Our total responsibility under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid in the 12 months before the problem. Neither of us is responsible for the other's indirect or lost-profit losses. Nothing in this agreement limits liability for intentional or gross fault, as Louisiana Civil Code article 2004 requires.

13. General terms. Louisiana law governs this agreement. Notices go by email: to lane@autoants.com for us, and to the email you signed up with for you. Changes to this agreement need both of us in writing, except the price and service-list changes it already allows with notice. Neither of us can transfer it to someone else without the other's OK, except as part of selling the whole business, with written notice. Each of us keeps the other's logins, customer data and private business information confidential and uses them only for this agreement. Neither of us is responsible for delays caused by events outside our reasonable control, such as a hurricane or an outage at Google, a phone carrier, Twilio or Vapi; we'll tell each other, and either of us can cancel if one lasts more than 30 days. If a dispute comes up, we'll first try to work it out for 30 days after written notice; after that, either of us can bring it in a state court in Orleans Parish, Louisiana. This agreement and the "Your Plan" page are our whole agreement; if they conflict, this agreement controls. If a court finds part of it unenforceable, the rest still applies. Electronic signatures count as originals.
