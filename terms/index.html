The autoants service agreement

Plain English. This is the agreement you sign when you start; the order page fills in your business and the services you chose.

AutoAnts LLC ("autoants") and [Your business's legal name] ("you") agree:

1. What autoants does. The services checked below, as described on autoants.com on the date you sign:

  • Try one: one assistant ($500 once, founding rate)
  • Get found and never miss a job ($1,000 setup + $1,250/month, founding rate)
  • Never miss a job ($750 setup + $750/month, founding rate)
  • Never miss a job, pay per job ($750 setup + $250/month + $25 per recovered job, never more than $750/month, founding rate)
  • Get found ($250 setup + $750/month, founding rate)
  • Get the next job add-on, with Get found and never miss a job (+ $500 setup + $1,250/month; $1,500 setup + $2,500/month in all, founding rate)
  • Assessment ($500, credited toward setup if you continue within 30 days; any part the setup fee doesn't use comes off your first month)
  • AI phone answering, on Get found and never miss a job (price and included minutes: ________________)
  • Operations Math monthly plan (priced by size, see Schedule A)
  • Other, scoped in writing: ________________

2. Payment. Setup fees are due when you say go. Monthly fees are billed in advance on the same day each month, starting on the day your first assistant goes live (not before). If a payment fails, we'll let you know and retry it over 7 days; if it's still unpaid after that, we pause the service until it's paid. Founding rates stay the same for as long as you keep the service.

3. The 7-day promise. Once you say go and give us what section 5 lists, your first assistant is live within 7 days, counted from the day the phone carriers approve your texting registration for anything that texts, or from go for everything else. If we miss it, that setup fee is refunded.

4. Term and cancelling. Monthly services run month to month. You can cancel any time after the first 90 days, effective at the end of the paid month. We can end the service with 30 days' notice.

5. What you provide. Accurate business information, logins or access where needed, and a reply within two business days when we ask you to approve something.

6. Your approval. Nothing is sent to your customers in a new form until you've approved the wording. You can change or pause any assistant at any time.

7. Texts and calls. Texts go only to people who contacted you first, or who agreed to hear from you; every text names your business and lets them reply STOP. You confirm you have that permission for any list you give us. Phone assistants say up front that they're AI. Text messaging requires registering your business with the carriers; you'll provide the information needed for it.

8. Your data and your site. Your customer information and your website content belong to you. We use them only to provide the services and never sell them (section 11). When the service ends, we delete them within 30 days without your needing to ask (section 11.7). You own your domain. If you leave, we hand over your site files.

9. Results. We'll do the work described and report the numbers honestly, but we can't guarantee a specific number of calls, jobs, reviews or rankings.

10. Using your name. We name you as a client or share your results only as section 11.4 allows.

10a. Creator credit (optional; check the ones you allow). Never on cover images or your main photos, and you can turn any of these off at any time by telling us:

  • Tag @autoants on social posts
  • "Video by autoants" line in YouTube descriptions
  • "Made with autoants" in small type on the last slide or end card only
  • "Marketing by autoants" in the email footer
  • One case study on autoants.com, which you approve before it's published

If you check at least 2 of these, your monthly fee is reduced by $25 while they stay on.

11. Data, publicity and public records.

11.1 Your data. We use your data, including your customers' information, only to deliver the services. We may also combine figures from many clients into de-identified, aggregated data (for example benchmarks and market reports) that never identifies you or any of your customers and always draws on at least 5 businesses. We own those aggregate outputs.

11.2 Selling or licensing data. We may license or sell de-identified, aggregated data made from client data (data that doesn't identify you, your business or any of your customers) without asking you again, and we keep what it earns. Data that identifies you or your customers is licensed only if you opt in: a separate written data licensing agreement (section 11.3), your approval of each deal, and 50% of what we receive paid to you. Otherwise we never sell or share it, except with the services in 11.6 that help us deliver your service.

11.3 Licensing your data (brokerage). If you choose to license data you own to a buyer (for example an AI company), that's a separate written agreement for each deal. You approve each deal before anything is shared, our fee is disclosed in writing, and we show you what you net after it.

11.4 Publicity. (a) Your marketing: in the marketing we make for you, we may use your name, logo, job photos and results. (b) Our marketing: our case studies, posts and website use your name, results or photos only with your OK on each piece. (c) Testimonials: only with your consent, in your own words. If you're on a founding or reduced rate, we say so next to it ("founding client, reduced rate"), as the FTC's endorsement guides require (16 CFR Part 255). (d) Your customers: their names, faces, homes and details are never shown without their consent. (e) Client list: we may list your business name among our clients. Tell us any time and we'll take it off.

11.5 Public-records products (Next Job Mailer, the parcel map, the data Ants). These are built from publicly available records. We're not a consumer reporting agency, and you may not use anything we give you to decide whether anyone is eligible for credit, insurance, employment or housing (Fair Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. §1681 et seq.). You won't use it to target, exclude or treat anyone differently because of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin or any other class the law protects (federal Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. §3601 et seq.; Louisiana Equal Housing Opportunity Act, La. R.S. 51:2601 et seq.). Anyone who asks not to be mailed is left off every client's mailings.

11.6 Services that handle data for us: Anthropic (AI assistant), Twilio (texts and calls), PostGrid or Lob (printing and mailing), Cloudflare (forms, hosting and storage), Google Workspace (email and files), OpenAI (a safety check on AI receptionist replies), Vapi (AI receptionist calls), Stripe (payments), DocuSeal (signing agreements), Fathom (recording calls with you, only with your OK), Telegram (call summaries to our phone) and the US Census geocoder (matching addresses). We may update this list; we'll tell you at least 14 days before a new one handles your data. If you object, tell us within those 14 days; if we can't serve you without it, you may cancel without the 90-day minimum applying.

11.7 Keeping and deleting. Within 30 days after the service ends, we delete your data and your customers' information automatically, without your needing to ask. If you want a copy, ask before then and we'll send it first. We keep only what the law requires (billing records) and the minimum needed to keep honoring opt-outs (a STOP or do-not-mail entry).

12. Limits. Our total responsibility under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid in the three months before the problem. Neither of us is responsible for the other's indirect or lost-profit losses. Nothing in this agreement limits liability for intentional or gross fault, as Louisiana Civil Code article 2004 requires.
