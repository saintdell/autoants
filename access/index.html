Google Business Profile

Time: ~2 min

What role to pick: ManagerNot Owner. A Manager can update your profile and answer reviews, but can't add or remove people or delete your profile.

On your phone (Google Maps app)

  1. Open the Google Maps app. Make sure you're signed in to the Google account that runs your business.
  2. At the bottom right, tap Business.
  3. Tap More (it may show as three dots), then Business Profile settings.
  4. Tap People and access.
  5. Tap Add.
  6. Type
  7. Under Access, pick Manager.
  8. Tap Invite. That's it.

On a computer

  1. Go to google.com. Make sure you're signed in to the Google account that runs your business.
  2. Search for my business. Your profile opens at the top.
  3. Click More (three dots), then Business Profile settings, then People and access.
  4. At the top left, click Add.
  5. Type
  6. Under Access, pick Manager, then click Invite.

We accept the invite on our side. Google may hold a few settings for 7 days after a new manager joins. That's normal.

Google's help page gives one path for every device. On a phone the "More" button can look a little different. If you can't find it, use the computer steps or do it with us on a call.

If you don't have an account yet: we'll set it up with you on the call.

Stuck? Reply to our email and we'll do it together on a 10-minute screen-share.

Prefer not to share your screen? We can talk you through it on a call, or do it with you in person. How we keep screen-shares private.

Facebook Page and Instagram

Time: ~2 min

What role to pick: partial accessNot full control. Turn on only what your plan needs: posting, insights (your numbers), and messages if we answer them for you. Leave ads and settings off.

Meta says to do this from Meta Business Suite on a computer.

Best way: add autoants as a partner

  1. Go to business.facebook.com and open Settings.
  2. Under Users on the left, click Partners.
  3. Click Add, then Give a partner access to your assets.
  4. Type our business portfolio ID. It's in our email to you. Click Next.
  5. Pick your Facebook Page and your Instagram account.
  6. Pick partial access and turn on only the tasks from the box above.
  7. Click Assign assets.

Other way: add us as a person

  1. In Meta Business Suite, open Settings, then People.
  2. Click Invite people.
  3. Type and click Next.
  4. Leave the business portfolio access at the basic level. Click Next.
  5. Pick your Page and Instagram account, with partial access only. Click Invite.

No Business Suite? Your Page alone

  1. On Facebook, switch into your Page.
  2. Go to Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Page setup, then Page access.
  3. Next to People with task access, click Add New, then Next.
  4. Type and pick us.
  5. Turn on only the tasks from the box above, then click Give Access.

Facebook may ask for your own password to confirm. Type it into Facebook only. Never send it to us. Don't see the Partners or People menu? You may not have full control of the portfolio. We'll find out who does, together.

Meta's pages don't list the task names in the same words everywhere, so we describe them in plain words. On the call we'll point to the exact switches.
