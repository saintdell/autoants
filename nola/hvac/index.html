How to hire: the Louisiana rules

What license does an HVAC company need in Louisiana?

Louisiana has no separate HVAC board. Heating, air conditioning and refrigeration work with a project value of $10,000 or more needs the LSLBC Mechanical license, and the HVAC subclassification sits under it. Smaller repairs may not need a state contractor license, so ask what the company holds.

Source: La. R.S. 37:2150.1(13) · LSLBC classification

What is EPA Section 608 certification?

Federal rules require anyone who opens a refrigerant circuit to pass an EPA-approved exam. It certifies people, not companies, so ask whether the technician coming to your house holds it.

Source: 40 CFR 82.161

What goes in an HVAC contract?

For licensed work, the written contract carries the contractor's name, address and license number, the work and the price. Work shouldn't start before it's signed.

Source: La. R.S. 37:2159

Check a license yourself: LSLBC: contractor license search

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. Is this job $10,000 or more? If so, what is your LSLBC Mechanical license number?
  2. Does the technician hold EPA Section 608 certification?
  3. Will the contract show your license number, the equipment, the work and the price?

What the work usually covers

AC and heating repair, system replacement, ductwork, maintenance plans and refrigeration.
