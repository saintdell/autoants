A badge for businesses in the New Orleans guide

If your business is in our New Orleans guide, you can put a small badge on your own website. It links to your listing, so your customers can see it. It's optional.

Listing is free, and the badge is optional. Nobody pays to be listed, and nobody has to show the badge. Putting it on your site, or taking it off, never changes whether you're listed, where you appear, or how your listing looks. We don't ask for it in exchange for anything.

What the guide is

The New Orleans guide is a free list of local trade businesses around New Orleans, made by autoants. It is built from public license records and business listings, shows facts a homeowner can check, and follows the order rules printed on each guide page. The badge isn't one of those rules. How the guide works.

Get the code for your listing

Badge style

    Copy the line and paste it into your website where you want the badge, usually the footer. If someone else runs your site, send them the line.

    Or build it by hand

    Open your trade's page in the guide and copy its address. Put it in place of the link in this line (the part after # points to your row; leave it off and the link still goes to your page):

    <a href="https://autoants.com/nola/hvac/jefferson/#l-your-business-name"><img src="https://autoants.com/badge/listed-light.svg" alt="Listed in the New Orleans Guide" width="180" height="60"></a>

    For the dark badge, change listed-light.svg to listed-dark.svg.

    Questions

    1. Do I have to show it? No. It's up to you, and nothing about your listing depends on it.
    2. Does it cost anything? No. The guide is free to be in, and so is the badge.
    3. Will it move my listing up? No. The badge never changes the order or how a listing looks.
    4. Can I take it down? Any time. Nothing changes on our side.
    5. Does it track my visitors? No. It's a plain image file with no script. You can also save it (light, dark) and host it yourself.
    6. Not listed, or something's wrong? Use the "Is this you? Claim or fix it" button on your trade's page, or email hello@autoants.com. Listings stay free.
