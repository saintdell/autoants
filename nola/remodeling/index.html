How to hire: the Louisiana rules

Which license does a remodeling contractor need in Louisiana?

It depends on the project value. Under $7,500 on a home, no LSLBC license. From $7,500 to under $50,000 with no structural work, a home improvement license. Structural work of $7,500 or more, or any home job of $50,000 or more, needs a Residential Construction license.

Source: La. R.S. 37:2150.1

Do the trades inside a remodel need their own licenses?

Yes. Plumbing, electrical and mechanical work are not covered by the general contractor's subcontractor exemption. Each person doing plumbing needs a plumbing license at any dollar value.

Source: La. R.S. 37:2157(A)(17) · La. R.S. 37:1366

What insurance should a remodeler carry?

Home improvement and residential licensees carry workers' compensation and general liability insurance. The liability minimum is $500,000 from August 1, 2026.

Source: La. R.S. 37:2156.1(E) · Act 757 of 2026

What goes in a remodeling contract?

The contractor's full name, address and license number, a description of the work, the total price, every party's signature and current insurance certificates. Each party gets a copy at signing, and no work starts before it.

Source: La. R.S. 37:2159

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. What is the project value, and which license covers it?
  2. Who does the plumbing, electrical and HVAC work, and under whose licenses?
  3. Can your insurance company send me your certificates?
  4. Does the contract show your license number, the scope and the total price?

What the work usually covers

Kitchens, baths, additions, whole-home renovations and structural repairs.
