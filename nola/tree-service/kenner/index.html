What's local in Kenner

  • To work in Kenner, contractors and tradesmen file for a city occupational license, and the application includes a copy of their state contractor or trade license. Asking a company whether it holds the Kenner license is a fair question.

    Source: City of Kenner, Contractors / Tradesmen, checked October 7, 2026

  • Kenner is in Jefferson Parish, which publishes its own list of licensed tree companies, good through March 31, 2027.

    Source: Jefferson Parish, 2026 Licensed Companies, checked October 2, 2026

Before you hire

Check the LDAF arborist license, get the work and the price in writing, and ask for an insurance certificate sent by the insurer. The full buyer's guide has the rules and the citations.
