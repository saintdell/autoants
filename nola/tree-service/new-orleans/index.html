What's local in New Orleans

  • Trees on city property, including the neutral grounds and the strips along sidewalks, belong to the city's Department of Parks and Parkways. Any work on a city tree needs its Tree Maintenance Work Permit, so a company asked to trim one should be the one to get it.

    Source: City of New Orleans, Parks & Parkways: Trees, checked October 7, 2026

Before you hire

Check the LDAF arborist license, get the work and the price in writing, and ask for an insurance certificate sent by the insurer. The full buyer's guide has the rules and the citations.
