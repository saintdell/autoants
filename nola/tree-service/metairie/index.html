What's local in Metairie

  • Metairie is unincorporated, so Jefferson Parish's rules apply directly. The parish keeps its own list of licensed tree companies, good through March 31, 2027, and the guide shows the parish license number wherever a company is on it.

    Source: Jefferson Parish, 2026 Licensed Companies, checked October 2, 2026

  • Jefferson Parish requires its own parish license to trim or remove trees, or to plant and maintain vegetation, on public property such as sidewalks, parkways, medians and parks. A tree service operator must first hold a state arborist license, and a landscaper a state horticulturist, landscape contractor or related license (Code of Ordinances sec. 37-20).

    Source: Jefferson Parish Code of Ordinances, ch. 37 (Vegetation), sec. 37-20, checked October 7, 2026

Before you hire

Check the LDAF arborist license, get the work and the price in writing, and ask for an insurance certificate sent by the insurer. The full buyer's guide has the rules and the citations.
