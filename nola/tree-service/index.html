How to pick the best tree service in New Orleans

  1. Working in Jefferson Parish? Ask for the parish license number. The parish publishes the list of licensed tree companies; check the number against it (the badges in this guide link to it).
  2. Ask who holds the state arborist license. Louisiana licenses arborists through the Department of Agriculture and Forestry's Horticulture Commission.
  3. Get proof of insurance from the insurer, not a photo of a card: liability, and workers' compensation if they have a crew. A fall or a dropped limb is the expensive kind of accident.
  4. Get the estimate in writing. Which trees and limbs, whether the stump gets ground, and whether the debris gets hauled away. Those three are where quotes quietly differ.
  5. After a storm, slow down. Be careful with door-knockers from out of town who want full payment up front. A local company with a license number and a phone that's answered can wait a day.
  6. ISA certification is a plus, not a license. It's a voluntary credential for a person, and you can verify it on the ISA's website.
