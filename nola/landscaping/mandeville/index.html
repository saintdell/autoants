What's local in Mandeville

  • Mandeville's tree removal application asks for the contractor's arborist license number, whether any live oaks, bald cypress or southern magnolias are involved, and the owner's agreement to the tree rules in Article 9 of the city's land use code (the CLURO).

    Source: City of Mandeville, Tree Removal Application (2025), checked October 7, 2026

Before you hire

Check the LDAF landscape horticulturist license, and if any hardscape is tied to the house, which contractor license covers it. The full buyer's guide has the rules and the citations.
