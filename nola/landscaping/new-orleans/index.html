What's local in New Orleans

  • Planting on city property, such as the strip between the sidewalk and the street, needs a Tree Planting Permit from the city's Department of Parks and Parkways before any tree goes in.

    Source: City of New Orleans, Parks & Parkways: Trees, checked October 7, 2026

Before you hire

Check the LDAF landscape horticulturist license, and if any hardscape is tied to the house, which contractor license covers it. The full buyer's guide has the rules and the citations.
