What's local in Gretna

  • The City of Gretna says every building trade professional except the general contractor must be licensed to work in the city, and it requires a permit to build, alter, repair or demolish. Its building department points homeowners to the LSLBC's search to check a contractor.

    Source: City of Gretna, Building Department, checked October 7, 2026

Before you hire

Check the LDAF landscape horticulturist license, and if any hardscape is tied to the house, which contractor license covers it. The full buyer's guide has the rules and the citations.
