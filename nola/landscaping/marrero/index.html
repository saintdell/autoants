What's local in Marrero

  • Jefferson Parish requires its own parish license to trim or remove trees, or to plant and maintain vegetation, on public property such as sidewalks, parkways, medians and parks. A tree service operator must first hold a state arborist license, and a landscaper a state horticulturist, landscape contractor or related license (Code of Ordinances sec. 37-20).

    Source: Jefferson Parish Code of Ordinances, ch. 37 (Vegetation), sec. 37-20, checked October 7, 2026

Before you hire

Check the LDAF landscape horticulturist license, and if any hardscape is tied to the house, which contractor license covers it. The full buyer's guide has the rules and the citations.
