Hiring Helper

It writes an honest job ad and a job page Google can show, collects short applications, and offers interview times. The owner makes every hiring decision.

The hard line: it never ranks, scores or filters applicants. Every application reaches the owner complete, in the order it arrived. The owner decides.

The ad, from a 20-minute intake call

HVAC Service Technician Sample Air Co · New Orleans, LA · Full-time Pay: $22-$30 an hour Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM, plus one on-call weekend a month in season You need
  • EPA Section 608 certification (Type II or Universal)
  • A valid driver's license (you'll drive a company van; we check the driving record after a job offer)
  • About 2 years of residential HVAC service work, or trade school plus 1 year
Nice to have (not required, so apply anyway)
  • NATE certification
  • Heat pump and ductless mini-split experience
Sample Air Co is an equal opportunity employer. We provide reasonable accommodation to apply, interview or do the job.

Wording check: clean

Before it's built, a wording check stops age words, gendered titles, health assumptions and pay-history questions, and flags record bans and lifting rules for the owner to decide. A real pay range is required. It's a wording check, not legal advice.

The job page on the owner's site

SAMPLE AIR CO · Careers
HVAC Service Technician $22-$30 an hour · Full-time · New Orleans Run 4 to 7 service and maintenance calls a day. You drive a stocked company van. Apply on this page: about 5 minutes, no resume needed.

The page carries Google's job markup, so it can show up in Google's job search. The owner (or we, on his account) pastes the same ad on Indeed and Facebook.
