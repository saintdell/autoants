Never miss a job

A whole day

Sam runs a made-up HVAC shop and spends the day on jobs. Here is everything Never miss a job does around him, from the first missed call to the monthly report.

The day, in order

  • Text-Back: a missed call gets one text.
  • Responder: a web form gets a drafted reply.
  • Scheduler: three open times; booked when Sam confirms.
  • Review ask: after the paid job.
  • Tracker: where today's calls came from.
  • Monthly report: the month on one page.

What it never does

Sends wording Sam hasn't approved, books a visit before he confirms, texts a call he answered, or asks the same customer for a review twice.

Price

Never miss a job: $750 a month + $750 setup, or $1,250 a month with Get found. 90 days, then month to month. Texts run on your own account.

Sample Heating & Air, Sam, Dana and Ray are made up, and so is every number, time and message (555 numbers). Set up by autoants.
