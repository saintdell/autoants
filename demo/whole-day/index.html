Catch missed calls

One day at an HVAC shop

Sam runs a made-up HVAC shop and is out on jobs all day. Press Play to see what happens on his customers' phones and his own. The demo stops whenever Sam has to decide, so tap his button to keep it going.

What you'll see

  • 8:05 AM, a missed call. Dana calls and nobody can pick up. She gets one text in Sam's approved words, and her reply goes to Sam. He texts her a time between jobs. (Text-Back)
  • 10:20 AM, a website form. Ray asks about a new system. A reply is drafted from answers Sam approved, and it waits until Sam taps Approve. (Responder)
  • 10:22 AM, booking. Ray gets three open times from Sam's calendar and picks one. It isn't booked until Sam confirms. (Scheduler)
  • 4:30 PM, a paid job. Sam is asked whether to send the review request. One tap sends it. (Review ask)
  • 5:00 PM, the day's count. Today's calls are matched to where each caller found the shop, along with the visits booked. (Tracker)
  • Once a month, one page. Missed calls texted back, jobs booked from them, money invoiced on those jobs and new Google reviews. (Monthly report)

What it never does

Sends wording Sam hasn't approved, books a visit before he confirms, texts a call he answered, or asks the same customer for a review twice.
