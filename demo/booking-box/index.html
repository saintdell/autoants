The three things the box can say

An open time
“Next available: …”, the earliest time that fits your hours, travel and job buffers, read from your own calendar.
Booked up
“Booked up for the next 14 days. Call us.” when nothing is open (14 days is a setting).
Can't read your calendar
“Call for times” when the calendar link fails, goes stale or has something it can't read. It never guesses.

What it never does

Shows a time your crew won't honor, or guesses when it can't read your calendar. It only reads your calendar; it never writes to it.
