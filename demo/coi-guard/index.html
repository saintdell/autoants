Expiring

Example Electric LLC · General Liability

Problem: Expires 2026-11-14 (12 days).

Action: Request the renewal certificate now, before it lapses.

If a field on a certificate can't be read, the report says it's missing. It never fills in a number.

Administrative review of certificates as furnished: not legal advice, not an insurance-coverage opinion, and no confirmation that the policies are in force. Coverage questions belong with the issuing agent or broker.

The email to the sub, drafted

To: office@example-electric.exampleSubject: Renewal certificate of insurance for Sample Roofing Co. Hi Example Electric team, Our records show the general liability policy on the certificate you sent us expires Nov 14, 2026. When it renews, could you have your insurance agent send us the updated certificate (ACORD 25), with Sample Roofing Co. as certificate holder like the current one? Thanks, Sam Sample Roofing Co.

What it never does: Fills in a field it can't read. An unreadable certificate is marked missing, never guessed. It's a document check, not legal advice or a coverage opinion.

Price: Scoped with you once you have Get found and never miss a job; not sold on its own.
