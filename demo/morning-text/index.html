Morning text

Every morning at 7, you get one text about yesterday: missed calls caught, new leads, quotes waiting and reviews asked for.

What it never does

Names a customer, or says 0 for something it doesn't track.

Price

Not on the menu right now. We built it and keep it ready; if your shop needs it, ask and we’ll price it with you.

What it counts

Only what the Ants log: missed calls and whether they got a text, new leads, quotes still waiting, review asks. Anything it can't see says “not tracked”, so a quiet day never looks like a blind spot.

Where it stands: Built, in practice mode. It needs the texting number and your OK to get texts from it.
