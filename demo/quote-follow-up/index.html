Never miss a job

Quote follow-up

When a quote goes quiet, it drafts a short check-in. It sends only after you say OK, and stops the moment the customer replies.

What it never does

Sends without your OK.

Price

Part of the Office Manager plan: $750 setup + $500/mo for the first 3 shops; then $1,500 setup + $750/mo.

The rules it keeps

  • Two check-ins at most (day 2 and day 7), then nothing.
  • No prices, discounts or deadlines in a check-in. The price stays in the quote.
  • Any reply, a booking, a no or a STOP ends it for good.

Where it stands: Built, in practice mode (nothing sends yet). It needs the texting registration (Twilio and 10DLC) first.
