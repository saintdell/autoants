How each number is counted

  • Calls and missed calls: your phone carrier's log.
  • Texted back and replies: the Text-Back send log.
  • Jobs booked: only the ones you confirm, line by line. A lead is never counted as a job.
  • Dollars: your own invoices, matched to the job. Nothing estimated.
  • Every number shows its source and the date it was read.

What it never does

Forecasts or projects a number, counts a job you didn't confirm, or shows a customer's name or phone number.
