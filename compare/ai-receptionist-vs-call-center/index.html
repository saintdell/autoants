When a call center is the better choice

  • You have a steady, high volume of calls that need people all day.
  • Calls need selling, long troubleshooting or judgment an AI shouldn’t make.
  • Calls need medical, legal or insurance answers. Ours never gives them.
  • Your callers won’t talk to an AI. Some won’t, and that’s their call.

When the AI receptionist fits

  • Calls ring out after hours, at lunch or while you’re on a job.
  • You want every call written up and texted to you, with the caller’s details.
  • You want a caller in a real emergency passed to a person at once, by rules you write.
  • It runs on its own or on top of any of our plans.
