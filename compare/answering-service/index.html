When an answering service is the better choice

  • Your callers expect a person every time, day or night. A text back is not the same as a voice.
  • Calls need judgment on the spot: an upset customer, a complicated job, a caller who won’t text.
  • You get only a few calls a month. PATLive lists a pay-as-you-go plan at $49 a month with no minutes included, then $2.99 a minute2: under our monthly price unless you use a lot of minutes.
  • You want calls booked by a person, in Spanish too. Ruby lists scheduling and Spanish and bilingual call handling1.
  • You already pay for a service that answers every call and books the work. Keep it.

When autoants fits

  • Calls ring out while you’re on a job and nobody calls back. A text goes back to the missed caller’s number automatically, at any hour (a landline can’t receive it).
  • Quotes go out and nobody follows up.
  • You want booking, reminders and the review ask in one place, on your own accounts.
  • You can use both: a live service for calls, and autoants for the text-back, the follow-ups and the booking.

Questions

Is autoants an answering service?

No. An answering service has people answer your calls. autoants texts back the calls you miss, follows up your quotes, and books and reminds appointments. A voice answer is our AI receptionist plan, and it always says it’s an AI.

Which costs less?

It depends on your minutes. Ruby starts at $250 a month for 50 minutes1 and PATLive at $49 a month with no minutes included, then $2.99 a minute2. Never miss a job is $750 a month + $750 setup for our first 3 shops.

Can I use both?

Yes. Some shops keep a live service for calls and use autoants for the text-back, follow-ups and booking.

Do you promise results?

No. We count your calls and jobs for one real week before we start, then show you the same numbers every month.
