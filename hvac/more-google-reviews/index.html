How does an HVAC company get more Google reviews without breaking Google's rules?

Ask every customer after every job, the same way, with a direct link, and never offer anything for the review. Google's rules ban rewarding customers for reviews and asking only the customers you expect to be happy.

This is a plain summary of public rules, not legal advice. The two differ in one place: the FTC rule allows some rewards for honest reviews, but Google doesn't allow any. On Google, follow Google.

A review ask that works for an HVAC shop

  1. Send it the day the job is done, while the house is cooling down and the visit is fresh.
  2. One message with your link, your business name and STOP to opt out, only to customers who gave you their number for the job.
  3. Ask every customer, including the callback and the one who complained. Skipping them is the gating Google bans.
  4. It's fine to say “if you can, mention what we did and your neighborhood.” Never write the review for them.
  5. Reply to every review within a day or two, bad ones too, in plain words. Never confirm private details about a customer in a reply.

Questions

Can I give a discount for a review?

Not for a Google review. Google's policy bans offering payment, discounts or free service in exchange for a review.

Can I ask only my happy customers?

No. Google's policy says not to ask selectively for positive reviews, and the FTC says that could violate the FTC Act. Ask everyone.

Can my techs ask for reviews?

Yes, the same way for every customer. Don't give techs a review quota or tell customers what to write; Google's policy bans both.
