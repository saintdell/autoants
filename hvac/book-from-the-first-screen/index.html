What a first phone screen that books has

  1. A call button you can hit with a thumb, top right, that dials the number it shows.
  2. A headline that says the service and the place: “AC repair and installation in Metairie”, not a slogan.
  3. Two reassurances, not six: the two your customers care about most, and only ones that are true.
  4. A Book or Request button, or a box with the next opening, before any scrolling.
  5. A time only if the crew keeps it. If the rule can't be checked, say “same-day when available”. A time that isn't real is the first promise a customer sees broken.

Check your own site in two minutes

  1. Open your homepage on your phone. Don't scroll.
  2. Can you tap to book or request a visit from what you see?
  3. Tap the call button. Does it dial the number printed on it?
  4. Send yourself a request through the form. Does it reach your phone, and how fast?

Questions

Isn't a phone number enough?

Keep the phone number first. But a customer who can't get through, or who wants to book at 10 PM, needs a way to ask for a time without a call.

Do I need scheduling software for this?

No. The box can show a time from your calendar or from a simple rule you set, and it says “same-day when available” when it can't check. It never shows a time your crew won't keep.
