What the answers read

  • Your own site: a page for each service and each town you serve, with plain answers to the questions callers ask.
  • Your Google profile: the right category, hours, services and photos, matching the site.
  • Listings: the directories the answers cite, with your name and phone the same everywhere. That includes Apple Maps, which Siri uses for places, and Bing Places.
  • Reviews: real ones, asked of every customer. In BrightLocal's US study, 80% of ChatGPT's answers to local searches cited Yelp. (How to ask inside Google's rules)

Measure it yourself

  1. Ask an AI assistant what your customers would ask: “My AC stopped blowing cold in Metairie. Who can come today?” Ask it three times and write down every company it names.
  2. Open the sources the answer cites. Those are the pages it trusted. Is your shop on them?
  3. Open Google Search Console for your site. Since August 31, 2026 it shows how often your pages appear in Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode.
