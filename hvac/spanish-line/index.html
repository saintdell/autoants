What a Spanish option means

  1. Spanish pages written for Spanish searches, such as “reparación de aire acondicionado en Kenner”, not a word-for-word copy of the English.
  2. Your Google profile services in Spanish, and the same facts in both languages: phone, hours, area served.
  3. The missed-call text and the review ask in Spanish, or in both languages.
  4. A person who reads Spanish reviews every wording before it goes out. Prices and promises stay the same in both languages.

What we won't do

  • Pick people by language, name or background. Area counts and your own channels only.
  • Call a page a “certified translation”.
  • Say “se habla español” for you unless someone at your shop does.

Questions

Is a translate button on my site enough?

It helps someone who is already on your site. A page written in Spanish, for the way people search in Spanish, is what a search engine or an AI assistant can find and quote.

Do I need someone on staff who speaks Spanish?

Not for Spanish pages or Spanish texts. Only say “se habla español” if a person who answers your phone does.
