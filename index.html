Straight answers

What owners ask first.

Is it really AI answering my customers?

Yes. It says it's an AI assistant at the start of every call, and that the call is recorded. It takes the job, the address and how urgent it is, and texts the request to you. It doesn't make promises on your behalf.

What if it gets something wrong?

Nothing gets confirmed to your customer without your OK: you reply yes with a time, or no. The first weeks run supervised, and every miss becomes a new rule so it doesn't happen twice.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your number stays the same. Calls you don't pick up forward to your assistant.

Do I own my website?

Yes. It's on your own domain and it's yours to keep, even if you stop paying for anything else.

What does it cost after the first month?

Site Care is $99 a month if you just want the site kept up. The Office Manager is $750 to set up and $500 a month at the founding rate. There's no contract to start, and monthly plans cancel anytime after 90 days.

What do you need from me?

Your Google Business Profile, about 20 minutes on the phone, and a few photos of real jobs if you have them. We build the rest.
