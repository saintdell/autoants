The rule above all of it

We don't automate dysfunction.

A broken process running faster is still a broken process — now it produces mistakes at machine speed. So the messy ones get fixed on paper first, and sometimes the honest prescription is a process fix and zero AI. Saying that out loud is exactly why the next recommendation is worth trusting.

The same rule has a second half: we never automate the relationship. The call where somebody decides whether to trust you stays yours. We automate the logistics around it.

Constraints we don't negotiate

What we won't do.

Most of this industry sells the opposite of this list. It's the most useful thing we can tell you before you spend money.

  • We won't manufacture your reputation.No written reviews, no bought ones, no "only ask the happy customers." Review gating and fake testimonials violate the FTC's Consumer Reviews Rule, and we'd be the ones who built it.
  • Our voice agent answers your phone. It never dials your customers.The FCC ruled in February 2024 that an AI voice is an artificial voice under the TCPA — outbound runs $500–$1,500 per call with no cap, and you'd be the one sued. We don't sell that product at any price.
  • We won't promise you a ranking, a lead count, or a revenue number.We'll help you measure what actually happens. Anyone quoting you a multiple before they've seen your numbers is guessing.
  • We won't work for free to get in the door.A small priced pilot removes your risk honestly and keeps both of us serious. The first artifact is free; the work isn't.
  • We won't automate something that should just be deleted.Sometimes the answer is a form, a checklist, or firing a subscription. You'll hear it.
