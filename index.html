All the questions

Is it really AI answering my customers?

Yes. It says it's an AI assistant at the start of every call, and that the call is recorded. It takes the job, the address and how urgent it is, and texts the request to you. It doesn't make promises on your behalf.

What if it gets something wrong?

Nothing gets confirmed to your customer without your OK: you reply yes with a time, or no. The first weeks run supervised. When it gets something wrong, I fix its instructions and tell you what changed.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your number stays the same. Calls you don't pick up forward to your assistant.

What does it cost?

Get Found + AI Front Desk is $1,250 a month, plus $1,250 to set up. Either one alone is $750 a month, and you can add Social Media later. AI phone minutes are priced with you before you sign. Three months, then month to month, cancel by email. Every price is on one page.

What do you need from me?

Your Google Business Profile, about 20 minutes on the phone, and a few photos of real jobs if you have them. I build the rest.
