Your first week

Guide 1

Send missed calls to your text-back

When you don’t pick up, your phone company passes the call to the number we give you. The caller gets one text from your business and can leave you a voicemail, which comes to you by text with their number.

Time: about 5 minThen a 30-second test

Before you start

  • Wait for our go. Turn this on only after we tell you your texting registration is approved. Before that, the carriers hold the texts, so callers would get no text and their voicemails wouldn’t reach you.
  • Have the number we gave you in front of you. We call it your text-back number. Forward only to that number.
  • You keep your number. Nothing is moved or transferred. Your phone still rings first, every time.
  • Your voicemail: while this is on, callers you don’t answer hear “Sorry we missed you. We just texted you; you can also leave a message after the tone.” Their message comes to you by text with their number and what they said (and by email with the recording, if you give us an address). Your old voicemail box stops getting new messages.
  • Have AI phone answering too? Same steps, with the number we give you for it. An AI answers the calls you miss and always says it’s an AI.

Find your phone company

Tap yours. Only “no answer”, “busy” and “unreachable” calls should forward, never all calls.

Cell phones

Verizon

  1. Open your phone’s keypad, the one you dial calls with.

  2. Dial *71 and then the 10-digit text-back number, with the area code. Example:

    *71 504 555 0123

  3. Press Call. Listen for the confirmation, then hang up.

What you’ll see when it workedYou hear a confirmation tone or message. Verizon calls this No Answer/Busy Transfer: your phone rings a few times first, then passes the call on.

To undo it: dial *73 and press Call. Verizon says the tone may sound like a busy signal.

Cost: Verizon says there’s no monthly fee for forwarding on most plans. If your plan doesn’t have unlimited minutes, forwarded calls are billed against your plan.

Checked against Verizon: Call Forwarding FAQs on 2026-10-19. Carriers change their menus; if a step looks different, ask us.

T-Mobile

Three short codes, one for each kind of missed call. Put a 1 in front of the 10-digit number.

  1. No answer: dial **61*1 then the number, then #. Press Call. Example:

    **61*15045550123#

  2. Busy: dial **67*1, the number, #. Press Call.

  3. Phone off or no signal: dial **62*1, the number, #. Press Call.

What you’ll see when it workedYour phone shows a short message after each code. The real check is the 30-second test below.

Want it to ring longer first? T-Mobile lets you pick 5 to 30 seconds. Add ** and the seconds before the # on the no-answer code. Example for 20 seconds:

**61*15045550123**20#

To undo it: dial ##004# and press Call. T-Mobile says this resets call forwarding to its normal setting.

Cost: T-Mobile’s terms say features like call forwarding can be charged as more than one call on your plan.

Checked against T-Mobile: self-service and short codes · T-Mobile terms and conditions on 2026-10-19. Carriers change their menus; if a step looks different, ask us.

Office lines and phone apps

Cox Business

  1. Go to myaccount-business.cox.com and log in.

  2. Under Services, tap the Voice icon.

  3. Open Call Settings, then Call Forwarding. (If you manage several lines, first pick the number under Users, Accounts & Call Settings.)

  4. Turn on Forward When No Answer. In Forward To, enter the text-back number. Pick how many rings before it forwards. Save.

  5. Do the same for Forward When Busy and Forward When Not Reachable.

What you’ll see when it workedThe rules show as On, with the text-back number beside them.

To undo it: same page, switch each rule Off and save.

You need to be the account’s owner or admin, or have permission from them. The Cox app can change these too.

Checked against Cox: Managing Call Forwarding in MyAccount · Cox: Changing Call Forwarding Rules Online · Cox: Forward When No Answer on 2026-10-19. Carriers change their menus; if a step looks different, ask us.

Spectrum Business

  1. Pick up your office phone and dial *92 (forward missed calls), then the 10-digit text-back number. Follow any prompts.

  2. Then dial *90 (forward when busy), then the same number.

What you’ll see when it workedSpectrum may give a short reminder ring on calls it forwards. That’s normal.

To undo it: dial *93 (missed calls) and *91 (busy).

Use the phone codes. In Spectrum’s online settings, the “Forward to Phone Number” choice sends calls on without ringing your phone first, which isn’t what you want here.

Checked against Spectrum Business: Call Forwarding features on 2026-10-19. Carriers change their menus; if a step looks different, ask us.

RingCentral

  1. In the RingCentral app, tap Settings, then Phone.

  2. Scroll to Call handling and tap Edit.

  3. Under Missed calls, choose Forward to external number, enter the text-back number, and Save.

What you’ll see when it workedMissed calls shows “Forward to external number” with the text-back number.

To undo it: same place, pick “Send to voicemail” again and Save.

If your company’s admin controls these settings, they make the change.

Checked against RingCentral: Setting up user call forwarding on 2026-10-19. Carriers change their menus; if a step looks different, ask us.

Quo (formerly OpenPhone)

  1. On a computer, open Quo and go to Settings → Phone numbers (under Workspace) → your number.

  2. Under Call flow, click Edit call flow.

  3. After the Ring users step, add a Forward call step with the text-back number. That way it only forwards when nobody picks up.

  4. Save and turn the call flow on.

What you’ll see when it workedYour call flow shows Ring users first, then Forward call.

To undo it: pick Default call flow and click Enable this call flow.

Cost: Quo says each forwarded call counts double toward your usage.

Checked against Quo: Call flows · Quo: Call forwarding on 2026-10-19. Carriers change their menus; if a step looks different, ask us.

Grasshopper

Grasshopper rings your numbers in a line. The text-back number goes last, after your own phone.

  1. Sign in to the Grasshopper admin portal. Select Extensions, then Edit on your extension.

  2. Select Add a forwarding number and enter the text-back number. For how to handle calls, choose “Calls will connect to you as soon as you pick up”. Select Add this number.

  3. On your own phone’s line, choose “Calls will be announced when you pick up”. Then a call you miss moves on to the text-back number instead of stopping in your cell’s voicemail.

  4. Under the forwarding numbers, select Options. Set “call each of my phone numbers” to In order, just once, with your phone first and the text-back number last.

  5. In Options, set the caller ID on forwarded calls to show the caller’s number, so the text reaches the person who called. Save.

What you’ll see when it workedYour extension lists your phone first and the text-back number second, set to ring in order.

To undo it: select Off next to the text-back number.

The True Solo plan allows only one forwarding number, so it can’t do this. Ask us about it.

The 30-second test (do this every time)

  1. Grab a different phone, not the business phone. A family member’s cell works.

  2. Call your business number from it. Don’t answer. Let it ring out.

  3. Within about a minute, that other phone should get a text from your business.

  4. On the same call, after the tone, leave a short message like “testing, please ignore.” Within a few minutes you should get a text with those words and the number you called from.

  5. Reply to our email: “It worked”, or tell us what happened.

If your phone didn’t ring first, or no text came, or your test voicemail never reached you, or a text went to the wrong phone: undo the forwarding with your carrier’s steps above, then tell us. Nothing is lost; your calls go back to normal.

Your off switch

If anything ever looks wrong, turn the forwarding off yourself with the undo steps above. Then tell us. Write your undo code on a sticky note by the phone.
