Privacy

How our screen-shares protect your privacy

A screen-share is optional. If we do one, you stay in control the whole time, and it’s short.

You pick how we do it

  1. First choice: do it yourself with the guide. Add hello@autoants.com as a user in your own account. The setup guides show you how.

  2. Second: a phone call. We talk you through it step by step. No screen-sharing at all.

  3. Third: in person. We’re local to New Orleans. We come by your shop and you do it on your own phone or computer while we sit with you.

If we do share a screen

  • You share, we only watch. We never ask for remote control of your computer or phone.
  • Share one window, not your whole screen.
  • Before we start: close your email, banking and texts, and turn on Do Not Disturb so messages don’t pop up.
Zoom: share one window

Click Share Screen in the meeting controls. Pick the one app window you need, not the whole desktop, then click Share. A green border shows exactly what we can see. Click Stop Share when you’re done.

Checked against Zoom: screen sharing security on 2026-10-19.

Google Meet: share one window

Click Present now at the bottom. Choose A window (or A tab), not “Your entire screen”, then pick the window.

Checked against Google Meet Help: present during a video meeting on 2026-10-19.

Microsoft Teams: share one window

Select Share in the meeting controls. Under Window, pick the one window. Select Stop sharing when you’re done. Don’t use “Give control”; we’ll never ask for it.

Checked against Microsoft: present content in Teams meetings on 2026-10-19.

On a phone, these apps share the whole screen, so closing other apps and Do Not Disturb matter even more.

Logins and codes

  • We never ask you to type a password while sharing. Stop sharing, sign in, then share again.
  • Two-step codes stay on your phone. Never read one to us. We’ll never ask.

Short, scheduled, not recorded

  • About 10 minutes, booked ahead. We send you the exact checklist before the call, so you know what we’ll look at.
  • We don’t record screen-shares.

What we keep

Nothing from your screen. We keep only the access you chose to give us, and you can remove it any time (how to remove our access).
