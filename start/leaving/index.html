Setup guide

If you ever leave

Time: ~20 minYou stay the owner

You own your accounts, pages, domain and customer list from day one. Leaving is about taking our access away and getting your files, and we walk you through it.

Cancel by email any time after the first 90 days. Service ends at the end of the month you’ve paid for.

1. Tell us

  1. Email hello@autoants.com.
  2. We reply the same day with the date your service ends and what happens to your number, your site and your data.
  3. We ask: “Do you want a copy of anything before we delete it?” Ask for your copy before the deletion date.

2. Your phone number

It never left you. We forwarded your missed calls; we never moved your number.

  1. We turn call forwarding off with you. On most phones: ##61# clears no-answer forwarding, ##67# clears busy forwarding, and ##004# clears all of them on many carriers. Codes vary, so we confirm yours with your carrier.
  2. We test it: call your number and let it ring out. It should reach your own voicemail, not ours.
  3. Then we release our forwarding number and switch off the AI line.
  4. Your texting registration was in your name. We switch it off, and you can register again with your next provider.
  5. Tracking numbers: keep them or let them go. If you don’t say, we release them.
You’ll seeA missed call goes to your own voicemail again.

3. Your accounts

Remove clients@autoants.com (or Lane) from each one, or tell us and we’ll remove ourselves where we can:

  • Google Business Profile: remove us as a Manager, and check that you’re the owner.
  • Search Console, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Nextdoor, and any other listing where you added us.
  • Facebook and Instagram: Page access or Partners (the messages guide shows where).
  • Your website builder: WordPress, Wix or Squarespace (the booking-box guide).
  • Your job software: Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan (the job-software guide).
  • Your calendar: stop sharing with us. In Google, press Reset next to the secret address; in Outlook, Unpublish (the calendar guide). We delete our saved copy of the address.
  • ManyChat and PostGrid are on your own card. Cancel them if you won’t use them.

4. Your domain and website

  • You already own your domain. We remove our access at your registrar. If we changed any DNS settings, we send you the current ones in writing first, so your next host can match them.
  • Your site files: a zip of the whole site (pages, styles, images, and notes on any forms) by email, or moved straight to your next host.
  • If we host your site, it stays up until your end date. Then we point your domain wherever you tell us. Never at a dead page.

5. Your customer list

It lives in your own job software, so it’s already yours. You can export it any time. If you want our copy of your customer data and content, ask before the deletion date. We send it as a zip by email or a link that expires, and we tell you what’s in it.

6. What we delete, and when

  • Everything we hold about your business and your customers, within 30 days after your service ends. Backups too.
  • We keep only the billing records the law requires, and the bare opt-out entries: a phone number that said STOP, an address on the do-not-mail list. No names or notes next to them. That way people who opted out stay opted out.
  • Totals that combine five or more businesses and can never point to you can stay. Operations Math files never go into those totals.
  • Want off our client list on the website? Just ask. A case study you approved stays only if your written OK still stands; we’ll ask you.

7. Our written confirmation

You’ll getAn email listing what we did and when: forwarding turned off and tested, site files sent, access removed, data deleted. If anything still shows us with access, tell us and we fix it the same day.

Stuck? Reply to our email and we’ll do it together on a 10-minute screen-share.
