Setup guide

Connect your job software

Time: ~10 minYou stay the owner

Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan. The easy way is to send us an export of your customers and jobs. The other way is to add us as a user.

We use your list for reminders, for postcards and emails to your past customers, and to test the assistants on your real records before they go live. Today we work from exports. A live connection to these programs isn’t built yet.

Three rulesNever send us a password. You stay the owner of the account. You can remove us any time.

Which way?

  • Send an export if adding a user would cost you a paid seat, or you’d rather keep it simple. Most shops do this.
  • Add us as a user if you’d rather we pull a fresh list each month. Use our email: hello@autoants.com.

Jobber

Send an export

Do this on a computer at Jobber.com.

  1. Click Clients in the left menu.
  2. Top right: More Actions, then Export Clients. Choose CSV. Jobber emails you the file. Over 1,500 clients come as several files.
  3. For your jobs, open the One-Off Jobs report (and Recurring Jobs if you have them). Click Export to CSV and pick all columns. Jobber emails it to your login email.
  4. Forward those emails to hello@autoants.com.

Or add us as a user

  1. Click the gear icon, then Manage Team, then Add User.
  2. Name: autoants. Email: hello@autoants.com.
  3. Permission level: Manager. It’s the lowest preset that can see reports, and it can’t add or remove your team or change your account settings.
  4. Click Save. Jobber sends us the invite.

A new user counts as one of your seats right away, even before we accept. Out of seats? Send the export instead. To remove us later: Manage Team, the three dots next to our name, Deactivate.

Housecall Pro

Send an export (this is the way for Housecall Pro)

Use a web browser, not the app. Only an Admin can export, so the owner usually does this.

  1. Click Customers in the left menu.
  2. Click Actions on the right, then Export. Check the email address and click Send file.
  3. Do the same from Jobs: Actions, Export, Send file.
  4. The files come from notifications@housecallpro.com within about an hour (check spam). Forward them to hello@autoants.com.

Adding us as a user (only if we ask)

Only Admins can export lists, and we don’t ask for Admin. So a user only helps if we’ve asked to look at your estimates with you.

  1. Click Team, then Team Members, then Add team member.
  2. Name: autoants. Email: hello@autoants.com. Housecall Pro needs a mobile number too: use the one in our welcome email.
  3. Role: Office Staff. Leave the extra permission boxes off unless we’ve asked for one.
  4. Click Save.

Checked against Housecall Pro: Import & Export Jobs and Customers, Housecall Pro: Manage Team Member Profiles, Housecall Pro: Team Member Roles & Permissions on 2026-10-19.

ServiceTitan

ServiceTitan accounts are set up differently from shop to shop. Your ServiceTitan admin may need to do these steps.

Send an export

  1. Open Reports and click the Customers report. Set the dates and click Run Report.
  2. Click Export, choose Export to XLSX, then Export. The file downloads to your computer.
  3. Do the same with the jobs report your office already uses, for the last two years.
  4. Email the files to hello@autoants.com.

Or add us as a user

  1. Go to Settings, People, Employees, and click Add.
  2. Name: autoants. Email: hello@autoants.com. Pick a username.
  3. Pick the role with the least access your office has. The role sets what we can see.

We couldn’t confirm from ServiceTitan’s help pages whether a new office user costs you a license. Ask your ServiceTitan rep first, or just send the export. To remove us later: edit our profile, Profile Actions, Deactivate employee.

What we do with your list

  • We use it only for your own customers: your reminders, your emails, your postcards.
  • We never sell it, pool it with other shops’ lists or add to it.
  • It’s yours. If you leave, we delete our copy (how leaving works).
