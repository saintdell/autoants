Setup guide

Add the book-now box to your website

Time: ~15 minYou stay the owner

A small box above your Book button: your next open time, read from your calendar, and a Book now button that opens the booking page you already use.

It uses your site’s font and your colors. No autoants logo. No cookies. It counts views and taps, with no names.

Before you start

  • Your calendar is shared (the calendar guide).
  • Your booking link: the online booking page you already pay for, or your request-service page.
  • Your rules: your hours, how long a first visit takes, travel time between jobs, how much notice you need, the most jobs you take in a day, the days you’re closed, and the phone number to show when you’re full.
  • One line of code from us. It starts with <script. Paste it exactly as we send it.

Easiest: we do it for you

Invite us with your website’s own invite, at the lowest level that works. Never your password.

  • WordPress: Users, Add New User, email hello@autoants.com, role Editor. On a regular WordPress site, Editors can save the box’s code. If it disappears when we save, change us to Administrator while we add it, then back.
  • Wix: Roles & Permissions, Invite Collaborators, hello@autoants.com, role Website Designer. Wix sites come with one free collaborator seat.
  • Squarespace: Permissions & Ownership, Invite Contributor, hello@autoants.com. Try Website editor first (it can add blocks to your pages). If it can’t add a Code block, switch us to Administrator while we add it.

Or do it yourself

WordPress (block editor)

  1. Edit your homepage.
  2. Right above your booking button, click the + (block inserter) and add a Custom HTML block. Or type /html and press Enter.
  3. Click Edit HTML, paste our line, and click Update. Then save the page.

On WordPress.com, the free plan strips code like ours out. It needs a paid plan with hosting features turned on.

Checked against WordPress: Custom HTML block, WordPress.com: Custom HTML block on 2026-10-19.

Wix

  1. Open the Editor. Click Add Elements on the left.
  2. Click Embed Code, then Popular Embeds, then Embed HTML.
  3. Click the new element, then Enter Code. Paste our line and click Apply.
  4. Drag it right above your Book button, then Publish.

Checked against Wix: Embedding a site or a widget on 2026-10-19.

Squarespace

  1. Edit the page. Right above your booking button, click Add block (or +) and pick Code.
  2. Click the pencil, paste our line, and leave Display Source off.
  3. Save the page.

Squarespace runs code like ours only on the Core, Plus or Advanced plan. On another plan the box won’t show. Tell us and we’ll talk it through.

When it worked

You’ll seeOn your phone, the box above your Book button with “Next open:” and a time, and Book now opens your booking page. We read you the time and ask: “Would you take a job then?” If not, we fix the rules first.

When you’re booked up

  • Tell us FULL by text or email. The box says “Booked solid today, call for the next opening” until midnight, then goes back to your calendar.
  • Tell us OPEN to bring it back sooner.
  • If your calendar can’t be read, the box says “Call for times.” It never guesses a time.

To take it off: delete the block from your page. Without the line, nothing shows.
