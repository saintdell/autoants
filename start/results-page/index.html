Setup guide

Read your monthly results page

Time: ~5 minYou stay the owner

Once a month, one private link shows what we did and what moved. It’s the same link every month; it just updates. Lane shares it with you.

Top to bottom

  1. Your measured week (the before). One real week we counted with you before we started: calls answered and missed by time of day, nights and weekends too; how many real leads got booked; cancellations. This is what “now” gets compared to.
  2. Recovered jobs (completed and paid). Each job an assistant caught, like a text-back reply, an after-hours answer or a quote follow-up, with its date and job number. On pay per job, your amounts and the monthly cap are here too. Think one is wrong? Email hello@autoants.com to dispute it.
  3. The numbers. Missed calls texted back, calls the assistant answered, minutes to first reply, leads received, jobs booked from the assistants, Google reviews and star rating, searches for your business name, searches where you’re on page two, website visits.
  4. Your assistants. Which ones are live or still setting up, and since when.
  5. What we did, Next, and What we need from you.

How to read a number

  • The big figure is the latest reading. Next to it is the change since the last one: green when it moved the good way, amber when it didn’t. Fewer minutes to reply is good; more is not.
  • Under each number: where it came from and the date we read it.
  • “Not tracked yet” means we aren’t measuring it yet, and the card says how it starts. A blank is never counted as zero.
  • Jobs booked counts only jobs you confirmed.

What it never shows

  • Your customers’ phone numbers or email addresses.
  • Forecasts, or promises about next month.
We don’t promise results. Some months a number goes down, and you’ll see that too. The page puts your before next to your now, with sources, so you can decide if it’s worth keeping.
