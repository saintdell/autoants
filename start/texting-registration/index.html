What we need to register your business for texting

The phone carriers block business texts from numbers that aren’t registered. We fill in the registration in your business’s name. You send us the details and say OK.

Time: about 10 min to gatherCarrier review: days to weeks

In plain words: “10DLC” is the carriers’ name for business texting from a regular 10-digit phone number. Every business that texts customers this way has to register who it is and what it sends.

Why the carriers ask

It cuts down on spam and scam texts. The carriers check that a real business is sending, and that people only get texts they asked for. Your texts run on a Twilio account in your business’s name, and the registration sits on that account.

Send us these

  1. Legal business name, exactly as on your IRS EIN letter (the CP 575 letter), with the same spelling, punctuation and “LLC” or “Inc.”. A photo of the top of the letter is the easiest way. Black out anything else on it.

  2. Your EIN (the 9-digit federal tax number on that letter).

  3. Business type: LLC, corporation, partnership, non-profit or co-op. And whether it’s private or public (almost every shop is private).

  4. Business address: street, city, state and ZIP.

  5. Your website. It has to work and show your business name.

  6. One contact person: first and last name, job title (owner, general manager…), email and cell number. A business email is best.

  7. Your OK in writing for us to register and text in your business’s name. A reply to our email saying so is enough.

Don’t send: passwords, bank details, or your Social Security number. We never need them for this.

What we fill in for you

  • How customers agree to get texts. They called your business first. They get one text back to the number that called, and they can reply STOP at any time.
  • Sample messages. We write them from the wording you approve (see approving messages). Every one names your business and says how to stop.
  • What kind of texting it is: replies to customers who contacted you. Never ads, never bought lists.

We show you the finished form before we submit it.

How long it takes

Twilio says the business check usually takes minutes, but a manual review can take seven business days or more. The texting review itself is currently taking 10 to 15 days, and a manual review can take two to three weeks. We start the day you send the details, and we tell you when it clears.

Twilio charges small registration fees to your account. We show you the current amounts before we submit anything.

What you’ll see when it workedAn email from us saying your texting is approved, with your text-back number. Then you turn on call forwarding and do the 30-second test.

Common snags

  • The name doesn’t match the IRS letter. Twilio requires an exact match. Copy it letter for letter.
  • A brand-new EIN. Twilio says a new EIN may not show up in the records yet; if it’s refused, we wait a few weeks and send it again.
  • No EIN, or no website? Tell us before you do anything else, and we’ll work out the right route with you.
