Setup guide

Connect Facebook and Instagram messages

Time: ~10 minYou stay the owner

So new messages on your Facebook Page and Instagram get a fast answer. We only ever reply to people who messaged you first.

  • Replies use wording you approved, and the first one says it’s your assistant and an AI.
  • No messages to anyone who didn’t write to you. No mass messages.
  • We never need your Facebook password, and you can remove us any time.

First: put both inboxes in one place

Your Instagram needs to be a professional (business) account connected to your Facebook Page. Then Instagram messages show up in the same Meta Business Suite inbox as your Page messages.

  1. On a computer, open Meta Business Suite and click Settings.
  2. Under Business assets, click Add assets, then Instagram account, and connect yours.
  3. When it asks, allow access to Instagram messages in the Inbox.

Already connected? Skip this.

Checked against Meta: Connect an Instagram account in Business Suite on 2026-10-19.

Then pick one way to add us

Way 1: add Lane to your Page (simplest)

  1. Go to your Facebook Page. Click Manage, then Page access.
  2. Next to People with task access, click Add New.
  3. Find Lane Davis II (the profile in our welcome email).
  4. Turn on only Messages and community activity. If we also make your posts, turn on Insights so we can report how they did. Leave Content and Ads off.
  5. Confirm. We accept the invite on our side.

Pick task access, not Facebook access. Task access lets us answer messages from Meta Business Suite. Facebook access would let us switch into your Page and run it, which we don’t need.

Checked against Meta: About Facebook Page access, Meta: Give, edit or remove Facebook Page access on 2026-10-19.

Way 2: partner access (if your business has a business portfolio)

You need full control of your business portfolio to do this.

  1. In Meta Business Suite, open Settings. Under Users, click Partners.
  2. Click Add, then Give a partner access to your assets.
  3. Enter our business portfolio ID from our welcome email and click Next.
  4. Pick your Page and your Instagram account. Choose partial access and only the messages task (plus insights if we make your posts). Not full control.

Checked against Meta: Give a partner access to business assets on 2026-10-19.

Meta changes these menus often, and its help pages wouldn’t load fully for us. We took the names above from Meta’s own help summaries. If yours look different, the screen-share takes ten minutes.

When it worked

You’ll seeOur name under Page access (or under Partners) with only the tasks you picked. We send a test message to your Page from a personal account, and you see the reply in your inbox.

To remove us

Page access, our name, Remove. Or Settings, Partners, our name, Remove.

Stuck? Reply to our email and we’ll do it together on a 10-minute screen-share.
