Setup guide

Share your calendar

Time: ~10 minYou stay the owner

The booking box and the scheduler look at your calendar to find your next open time. They only read it. Nothing we run changes your calendar.

A booking comes to you as a draft you add yourself. We never need “make changes” access.

Two ways to share

  • The private calendar address. This is what the booking box uses to show a real open time. We copy it together on a screen-share.
  • Sharing with our email. This lets us look at your free and busy times before we offer a customer a time.
Why we copy the private address together: anyone who has it can see your calendar. Google says not to share it with other people. So it never goes in an email or a text. We paste it from your screen straight into locked storage, and you can kill it any time with one button (below).

Google Calendar

The private address (on the screen-share)

On a computer, at calendar.google.com.

  1. Click Settings (the gear, top right).
  2. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click the calendar your jobs are on.
  3. Click Integrate calendar. Scroll to Secret address in iCal format. Stop there. We copy it together.

Don’t see it? On a Google Workspace account, your admin may have turned it off. Your admin has to allow it.

To take it back: the same screen, then Reset next to the secret address. The old address stops working at once.

Share with our email

  1. On the left, under My calendars, click the three dots next to your calendar, then Settings and sharing.
  2. Click Shared with, then Add people and groups. Type clients@autoants.com.
  3. Pick See only free/busy (hide details). That’s all our booking setup needs. Never pick any “Make changes” option.
  4. Click Send.

To stop sharing: Settings and sharing, Shared with, then remove us.

Checked against Google: Get your calendar (secret address), Google: Share your calendar with someone on 2026-10-19.

Outlook

We haven’t tested an Outlook calendar with the booking box yet. We’ll test yours before we rely on it.

Share with our email (Outlook on the web)

  1. Open the calendar. On the toolbar, click Share and pick your calendar.
  2. Type clients@autoants.com.
  3. Pick Can view when I’m busy. Never Can edit or Delegate.
  4. Click Share.

The published address (on the screen-share)

  1. In the calendar, open Settings, then Shared calendars.
  2. Under Publish a calendar, pick your calendar and how much detail to show. We’ll pick it with you.
  3. Click Publish. We copy the ICS link together.

To take it back: Settings, Shared calendars, Unpublish.

Checked against Microsoft: Share your calendar in Outlook on the web on 2026-10-19.

Jobber

  1. Open Jobber.com in a browser (not the Jobber app). Calendar syncing only works there.
  2. Go to Calendar Syncing and tick what to include: visits, requests, and the items that are unassigned or assigned to everyone.
  3. Use the owner’s or the dispatcher’s link, whichever shows every crew. We copy it together on the screen-share.

Jobber’s help doesn’t say how to reset this link. If you ever want it shut off, we’ll ask Jobber support with you.

Checked against Jobber: Calendar Syncing on 2026-10-19.

Housecall Pro

Housecall Pro has no calendar address of its own. It can copy your jobs into Google Calendar, and we read that.

  1. In a web browser, go to My Apps, then All Apps, and open Google Calendar.
  2. Click the toggle at the top right, then Connect. Your jobs, estimates and events start showing in Google.
  3. Then follow the Google steps above for that calendar.

This needs web access, and it doesn’t work for a Field Tech login.

Checked against Housecall Pro: Integrate with Google Calendar on 2026-10-19.

When it worked

Stuck? Reply to our email and we’ll do it together on a 10-minute screen-share.
