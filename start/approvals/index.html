Approving messages by text

Nothing goes to your customers until you’ve said yes to the wording. Here’s how you say yes, how you change something, and what happens if you don’t answer.

Time: about 3 min to read

1. The wording, once, before anything goes live

  1. We send you every message each assistant can send, word for word.

  2. Reply OK to approve them, or tell us what to change in your own words. We send back the new version.

  3. Your reply is your written approval. We record who approved it and when.

If we change even one word later, it comes back to you before it can be used. The system blocks any message whose wording you haven’t approved.

We ask for an answer within two business days. If you don’t answer, that message doesn’t go out. Nothing is sent on silence.

2. New drafts, like your weekly Google post

Things that change each time, like a Google profile post or a seasonal campaign, come to you by text as a draft or a link to look at.

Reply YES to approve, or reply with what to change. If it comes as a link to a review page, mark each piece Approve or Needs change there. No answer means it waits; it never goes out on its own.

3. Job requests, if you have AI phone answering

When an AI answers a call you missed, it takes the job details. It never books, quotes or promises anything. You get one text with the job, the address, the times the customer asked for, their number and a 4-character code.

To confirm a time, reply Y, the code and the time:

Y K7QM Tue 9am

To pass, reply N and the code:

N K7QM

  • Only a Y reaches the customer, and only if they said yes to texts on the call. Otherwise we tell you to call them.
  • A thumbs-up or “like” on the text doesn’t count. Reply with the code.
  • If you don’t reply, the customer isn’t told anything. You can always just call them.

When customers text back

Their reply comes to you word for word, labeled with what it’s about. You answer them yourself. The assistants never answer a customer on their own, except to confirm a STOP or send your approved help message.

Quiet hours

  • Your customers get texts only between 8 AM and 8 PM your time, unless you choose a different window inside 8 AM to 9 PM. A text due at night waits for the morning.
  • You get texts between 6 AM and 9 PM by default. A customer reply that comes in at night reaches you in the morning.

How to pause

  • Missed-call texts, right now: turn off call forwarding with your carrier’s undo code (the guide).
  • Anything else: reply to our email or call us and say pause. You can pause or change any assistant at any time.
  • There’s no PAUSE text word for you. A customer who texts STOP is never texted again.
