Setup guide

Send win-back emails from your domain

Time: ~15 minYou stay the owner

Emails to your past customers go out from your own address, like you@yourshop.com, signed by you. For that, your domain needs three settings, so inboxes trust the mail.

The three settings, in one line each

  • SPFA list of who is allowed to send email for your domain.
  • DKIMA signature on each email that proves it came from you and wasn’t changed on the way.
  • DMARCTells inboxes what to do with mail that fails those checks, and sends you reports.

What we send you

Two or three short entries, from the email service that sends for you. Each one has a type, a name and a value. Copy each one exactly.

  • SPF: type TXT, name @. If your domain already has an SPF entry (it starts with v=spf1), we’ll send you an edited one. Don’t add a second.
  • DKIM: type TXT or CNAME, with a name ending in ._domainkey.
  • DMARC: type TXT, name _dmarc.

Add them where your domain’s DNS lives. Usually that’s where you bought the domain, unless your web host or Cloudflare runs it. If what you add never shows up, the DNS lives somewhere else, and we’ll find it together.

GoDaddy

  1. Sign in and open your Domain Portfolio.
  2. Next to your domain, click Domain Edit Options, then Edit DNS.
  3. Click Add. Pick the type (TXT or CNAME), then fill in the name and value we sent. Leave the TTL as it is.
  4. Click Add Record. Repeat for each entry.

Namecheap

  1. Sign in. Click Domain List, then Manage next to your domain.
  2. Open the Advanced DNS tab and click Add new record.
  3. Pick the type. In Host, type the name we sent, without your domain on the end. In Value, paste the value.
  4. Click Save all changes. It usually takes about 30 minutes to work.

Squarespace Domains

  1. Open your domain in Squarespace. Click DNS, then DNS Settings.
  2. Scroll to Custom Records and click Add record.
  3. Pick the type. Type the name we sent (use @ if we didn’t send one), and paste the value.
  4. Save. Repeat for each entry.

Checked against Squarespace: Edit your domain’s DNS records, Squarespace: Add DNS records for email on 2026-10-19.

Cloudflare

  1. Sign in and pick your domain. Open DNS, then Records.
  2. Click Add record. Pick the type, then fill in the name and the content we sent.
  3. For a CNAME, set Proxy status to DNS only (the grey cloud). TXT entries are always DNS only.
  4. Save. Repeat for each entry.

The email rules (CAN-SPAM), in plain words

The law covers you even when someone else sends for you. So every email we set up has:

  • Your business’s name in the From line, on your own domain.
  • An honest subject. No fake “Re:” or “Fwd:”.
  • A line saying it’s a marketing email.
  • Your business’s postal address. A PO box is fine.
  • A working unsubscribe. Anyone who unsubscribes is never emailed again, well inside the 10 business days the law allows.

And our own rules on top:

  • Only your own customers, at addresses they gave you. No bought lists.
  • Email only. No texts or calls to old customers from this.
  • You read and approve every email before it goes out, and a person presses send. Small batches first, so your domain stays in good standing.
