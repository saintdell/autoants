Find your Google review link

Google gives every business a link that opens the review box. We use your own link, copied from your profile, never one we make up.

Time: about 3 min

Sign in with the Google account that manages your business profile first. In Google’s help the button is called Get more reviews. Some older screens called it “Ask for reviews”.

On your phone

  1. Open the Google Maps app and tap Business at the bottom right. Or search Google for my business.

  2. Find Read reviews, then tap Get more reviews.

  3. Tap Copy.

On a computer

  1. Search Google for my business, or your business name and city.

  2. Select Read reviews, then Get more reviews.

  3. Select Copy. The QR code for printing is there too (Google only makes it on a computer).

What you’ll see when it workedPaste the link in a text to yourself and tap it. It should open the review box for your business.

Send it to us

Paste the link into a reply to our email. That’s all we need. Please don’t send your Google password.

One rule from Google

Google prohibits offering anything for a review, like a discount or a freebie. Our review ask never does, and it asks every customer the same way.
