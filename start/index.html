Your first week with autoants

Five short jobs on your side. Each one has a plain guide. Most take a few minutes on your phone.

Time: about 30 min in all
  1. Give autoants accessAdd clients@autoants.com as a user in your own accounts. You keep owning everything.
  2. Send your texting registration detailsThe carriers need your business registered before we can text in your name. Start this first: it takes the longest.
  3. Send us your Google review linkCopied from your own Google profile.
  4. Learn how approving worksNothing goes to your customers until you’ve said yes to the wording.
  5. Send missed calls to your text-backDo this when we tell you your texting registration is approved. Then a 30-second test.

Three things that stay true

  • You own every account. Your phone number, your Google profile, your texting account and your customer list stay yours.
  • We never ask for a password. You add us as a user, or you do it yourself while we talk you through it. If anyone asks for your password in our name, it isn’t us.
  • You can switch it off. Each guide tells you how to undo what you set up.

How our screen-shares protect your privacy

Stuck? Reply to our email and we’ll do it together on a 10-minute screen-share.

Rather not share your screen? We can talk you through it on a phone call, or come by your shop. How our screen-shares protect your privacy.
