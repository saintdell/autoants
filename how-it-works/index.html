How it works

From a free mini assessment to more customers, step by step.

You know your business. I know today's AI tools. Here's exactly what happens after you book, what you do, and what I do.

  1. 1

    The free mini assessment. A written report on your business, made only from public information: the questions customers ask Google and ChatGPT about your kind of business, who gets named today, and the first fixes I'd make. Yours to keep. See a sample.

    You: nothing. It's ready before we talk.

  2. 2

    The 15-minute call. We go through the mini assessment together and pick the fix. You pick one fix or both.

    You: 15 minutes, by phone or in person.

  3. 3

    Setup. One setup call, then I build it from your facts and test it. You see every message it can send before anything goes live. Setup, step by step.

    You: a 30–40 minute call, and approving the wording.

  4. 4

    Live. Your Google profile is kept right and posted to every week, calls and texts get answered, and quotes get a follow-up. You get the jobs by text to confirm.

    You: confirm the jobs you want.

  5. 5

    The 30-day receipt. One page: calls caught, quotes followed up, reviews gained, each number with where it came from. The ones that didn't move stay on the page too.

    You: read it. I don't promise results; I count them.

  • Any time: call or text me. I reply within one business day, usually the same day.
