What owners ask first.

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, call (504) 499-1194 or get in touch.

What does autoants do?

I'm Lane Davis II, an AI consultant in New Orleans. I find where today's AI tools can bring your business more customers, then I set them up. Most fixes are one of three: Get Found (show up on Google and in ChatGPT's answers), AI Front Desk (every call and text answered, quotes followed up) and Social Media (posts and short videos from your real jobs).

Who's behind autoants?

autoants was founded by Lane Davis II in New Orleans. He signs off on every setup, and you deal with him directly, not a call center. About Lane.

What is the free mini assessment?

A short report on your business, made from public information: how customers find you on Google and ChatGPT today, and where today's AI tools fit. We go through it together in 15 minutes. You don't fill anything out, and it's yours to keep.

What does it cost?

Get Found + AI Front Desk is $1,250 a month, plus $1,250 to set up. Either one alone is $750 a month plus its setup. Social Media adds $1,250 a month. Prices are for our first 3 shops with up to 15 trucks; bigger or multi-location operations are quoted by size. Every price on one page.

Is there a contract?

No 12-month contract. Monthly plans run three months, then month to month, and you cancel by email. Pay for a year up front and setup is free.

Is it really AI answering my customers?

Yes. It says it's an AI assistant at the start of every call, and that the call is recorded. It takes the job, the address and how urgent it is, and texts the request to you. It doesn't make promises on your behalf.

What if it gets something wrong?

Nothing gets confirmed to your customer without your OK: you reply yes with a time, or no. The first weeks run supervised, and every miss becomes a new rule so it doesn't happen twice.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your number stays the same. Calls you don't pick up forward to your assistant.

Do I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your domain, your website, your Google profile and every lead are in your business's name. If you stop, I hand everything over. Your data.

Do you promise results?

No. I don't promise calls or rankings. I measure where you are now and show you the same numbers later, in a one-page 30-day receipt: calls caught, quotes followed up, reviews gained, each with where it came from.

Will it text or call people who never contacted me?

No. The assistants reply to people who called or wrote to you first, or who said yes to texts from you. I never cold-call or cold-text anyone on your behalf. Texts and calls.

Do I have to switch software?

No. You keep the tools you already use. On the call I'll tell you plainly whether I can connect to yours.

What do you need from me?

Your Google Business Profile, about 20 minutes on the phone, and a few photos of real jobs if you have them. You approve every word before anything goes live.

Will you work with my business?

Probably, if customers find you on Google and call or text you. Good fit and not a fit yet.
