How Spectora reports are laid out

Spectora reports usually open with a summary, then go system by system. Items are numbered by section and item (for example 4.1.2), and each defect carries a category the inspector chose, often labeled along the lines of safety hazard, recommendation or maintenance item. Inspectors can rename those categories, so we keep whatever label your inspector used.

How we read them

  • We read the numbered items in the body of the report and skip the repeated summary list, so nothing is listed twice.
  • Each item keeps its number, its system and component, the title the inspector gave it and the inspector's category.
  • Photos placed under an item stay with that item on your list.

What you get

  1. Every item in your inspector's words. We quote the report word for word. We don't reword it, shorten it or add our own opinion.
  2. Grouped by trade. Roofing, plumbing, electrical, heating and air, structural and foundation, pest and termite, general handyman and more, so each contractor gets the items they do.
  3. Safety items first. Items your inspector rated or described as safety items come first, then repair items, then monitor and maintenance items. The rating shown is always the inspector's.
  4. What to ask each contractor. License, insurance, a written price item by item, permits, timing, plus a few trade-specific questions.
  5. A printable PDF and a checklist you can share with your agent, family or contractors.
