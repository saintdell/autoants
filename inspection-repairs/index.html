Turn your inspection report into a repair list by trade

A 40-page report is hard to hand to a roofer, a plumber and an electrician. We pull out each item your inspector flagged and sort it by the kind of contractor who usually does that work.

What you get

  1. Every item in your inspector's words. We quote the report word for word. We don't reword it, shorten it or add our own opinion.
  2. Grouped by trade. Roofing, plumbing, electrical, heating and air, structural and foundation, pest and termite, general handyman and more, so each contractor gets the items they do.
  3. Safety items first. Items your inspector rated or described as safety items come first, then repair items, then monitor and maintenance items. The rating shown is always the inspector's.
  4. What to ask each contractor. License, insurance, a written price item by item, permits, timing, plus a few trade-specific questions.
  5. A printable PDF and a checklist you can share with your agent, family or contractors.

How it reads your report

Most inspection software exports a PDF with a summary, numbered sections and a rating on each item. We read the layout of the common formats directly, and fall back to a general reader for the rest. Every item keeps its section, location, rating and photos when the report has them. If we read an item with lower confidence, the list says so and asks you to check it against the report.

What happens to your report

  • Your PDF is processed in a private temporary folder and deleted as soon as your list is built.
  • The seller's and the inspector's names, phone numbers, emails and license numbers are left out of your list.
  • Nothing about you is kept unless you choose to ask for bids (below).

Want bids?

Your checklist has a Get bids from licensed, insured local pros button. Lane Davis II's Referral Desk matches up to three contractors per trade who hold the state license the work needs and have liability insurance on file. You see their names first. Nothing is shared with any contractor until you say yes to that contractor by name, and only the items you pick. Contractors on the list pay Lane Davis II a referral fee; that never changes who's shown or what you pay.
