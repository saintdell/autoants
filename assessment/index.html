Straight answers

Do you promise results?

No. The report shows what I found and what each fix is worth in your own numbers. What happens after that depends on your business, and I won't promise an outcome I can't control.

Can you get me to the top of Google or ChatGPT?

No one can honestly promise that; the answers change from one search to the next. I measure where you show up today and what feeds those answers, and the report says what would help.

Is this a sales pitch?

It's a paid report you keep, whether or not you hire me. If your numbers are small, it says so and recommends the smaller option, or doing it yourself.

Is the interview recorded?

Only with your OK. The recording is used to write your report and isn't shared.

Do the AI tools talk to my customers as if they were a person?

No. Anything we set up that talks to your customers says it's an AI, and nothing goes out in your name without your approval.

Do I need to give you passwords?

No. You keep every account. For the assessment I only need the conversation and the numbers you choose to share.
