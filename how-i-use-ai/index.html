How I use AI

I keep up with AI so you don't have to.

I'm on the AI tools every day, looking for edges a New Orleans business can use. Here's what I use, and what I'm watching for local businesses right now.

What I use every day

  • Claude (Anthropic): my main tool for research, writing and building.
  • ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google) and Grok (xAI): second opinions, and checking what each one says about a business when customers ask it.
  • Meta AI: what owners and customers see inside Facebook and Instagram.
  • The phone and texting behind AI Front Desk: a voice-AI system that answers on your existing number, and a texting provider registered in your business's name.
  • Google Business Profile, Apple Business and Bing Places: where customers find you on maps.

No one pays me to list these. I switch tools when a better one shows up; that's the point of keeping up.

What I'm watching this month

  • Nov 25, Google Maps: a Message button now sits next to Call on your listing. Somebody has to answer those texts.
  • Nov 10, ChatGPT: owners can claim their business profile (beta), so its answers get your hours and services right.
  • Apple Maps: Apple Business replaced Business Connect. If you claimed your listing a while back, the place you manage it moved.
